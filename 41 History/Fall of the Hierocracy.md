# Description

Also called the "War of Loss", the Sunmaker aka Sadees (Alethi warlord) revolted against Vorin rule. Sadees split Vorinism into Devotaries, decentralizing powers, and stripped ardents of the rights to own property and political office. Their "visions of the future" were debunked and casting doubt on fortune telling.

He later conquered Herdaz, Jah Keved, all the way to Azir. After his death the kingdom was split into ten princedoms.
