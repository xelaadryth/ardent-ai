# Description
A mysterious, white-haired traveler and storyteller who appears at pivotal moments. To the Nurians, he was known as "Loid" due to their specific linguistic patterns, but as the party integrates into Alethi society, they recognize his name as Hoid.

# Personality
Hoid is a master of wit, insult, and performance. He speaks in riddles and metaphors, often providing guidance that is only understood much later. He enjoys manipulating those who believe they are in control, particularly [[Sweeper]], whom he uses as a pawn to subtly steer the party's path.

# Background
Hoid was present during the [[000 Arrival]] of the Nurian refugees, where he recounted the tale of [[The Candle and the Mirror]]. He appears to possess knowledge far beyond that of a standard traveler, including insights into the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]].

# Goals
- Observe the emergence of new Radiants on Roshar.
- Manipulate regional events to ensure certain outcomes.
- Collect experiences and stories from across the Cosmere.

# Connections
- [[Sweeper]]: His local contact in [[Revolar]]. Hoid often uses him to "leak" information to the party.
- [[N'tal]]: Vetted his motives during their arrival.
- [[Iiko]]: Witnessed his storytelling at the camp.
