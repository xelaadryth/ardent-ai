# Description
A Scadrian Ghostblood agent who appears Shin in stature, possessing dark eyes. He is a Twinborn (Rioter and Pewter Feruchemist) who has been organizing resistance efforts.

# Personality
Action-oriented, pragmatic, and fiercely loyal to the cause. He works well with [[Koloko]], whom he invited to join the party.

# Background
A key recruiter for the rebellion who helped facilitate the liberation of [[Peakspren]] and the gathering of intelligence in [[Rekka]]. He assisted [[Viscose]] in enhancing [[Galeth-son-Thald]] via a Hemallurgic spike.

# Goals
- Liberate [[Haka'alaku]].
- Dismantle [[The Set]]'s operations in Shadesmar.

# Connections
- [[Koloko]]: Ally and recruitment target.
- [[Ghostbloods]]: Faction affiliation.
