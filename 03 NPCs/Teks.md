# Description
A grizzled Alethi deckhand who has spent too many years on the sea and has little patience for the influx of "outlanders."

# Appearance
Leathery skin, missing several teeth, and always smells of salt and cheap tobacco. He wears a faded red bandana and keeps a short, heavy club at his belt.

# Personality
Grumpy, suspicious, and openly hostile toward anyone who doesn't look "properly Alethi." He view the Nurian party members and the Shin [[Galeth-son-Thald]] as bad omens and potential spies for the [[Truthkeepers]].

# Background
Teks lost his previous ship to a worldhopper pirate raid (which he mistakenly blames on all foreigners). He stays on the [[Deepen Light]] because Varo pays well and doesn't ask questions about his past.

# Goals
- Ensure the ship isn't "cursed" by the presence of the party.
- Collect enough marks to retire to a small farm far from the coast.

# Connections
- [[Varo Three-Knives]]: His employer, whom he respects but thinks is too soft on outsiders.
