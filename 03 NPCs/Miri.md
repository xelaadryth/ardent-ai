# Description
A young, bright-eyed Thaylen lookout with an insatiable curiosity about the wider Cosmere.

# Appearance
Short with long, white Thaylen eyebrows that she occasionally braids. She is incredibly agile and spends most of her time in the crow's nest of the [[Deepen Light]].

# Personality
Exuberant and friendly. She is fascinated by the party, especially their varied heights and eye colors. She often peppers them with questions about their homelands and their experiences with the [[Truthkeepers]].

# Background
Miri ran away from a restrictive apprenticeship in [[Thaylen City]] to see the world. She joined Varo's crew after he impressed her with a display of his three-knife technique in a tavern.

# Goals
- Learn the truth about the "[[Possessed]]" and the return of the Radiants.
- Sail to every ancient city (dawncity) on Roshar.

# Connections
- [[Varo Three-Knives]]: Her mentor and Captain.
- [[Kuma]]: Intrigued by his Nurian fabrials.
