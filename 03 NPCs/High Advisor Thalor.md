# Description
A high-ranking advisor in the Nurian court who acted as the primary administrative gatekeeper for the King. He was deeply embedded in the politics of the major noble houses.

# Personality
Calculated, obstructive, and manipulative. Thalor excelled at managing the King's laziness to ensure noble interests—including his own—remained secure. He effectively isolated the monarch from the reality of the merchant class.

# Background
Thalor held the real administrative power behind the throne, handling all paperwork that the King found too tedious to manage. He was instrumental in blocking inquiries into suspicious land dealings and gem mine seizures, including the [[Aelwood Contract]]. He ensured that documents signed by members of the royal family, such as [[Fina]], were pushed through without scrutiny, and he would have actively suppressed any attempts to investigate or delay these deals.

# Goals
- Maintain the dominance of the high noble houses.
- Enrich himself through brokered deals and land seizures.
- Prevent the royal family from conducting independent oversight.

# Connections
- [[Fina]]: He served as the handler for her administrative duties, ensuring her signatures advanced noble agendas.
- [[N'tal]]: A merchant whose family he ultimately betrayed by facilitating the seizure of their lands.
- [[Beric Quinn]]: A hunter who deeply resented Thalor’s role in the corrupt aristocracy that neglected his people.
