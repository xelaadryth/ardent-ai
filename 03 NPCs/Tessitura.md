# Description
A quick-witted and rebellious Lightspren navigator who escaped the [[Bloodmarks]] blockade of [[Haka'alaku]].

# Personality
Tessitura is a spark of defiance. She is restless and finds the stagnant life in [[Rekka]] suffocating, often taking unnecessary risks to buzz around the Scadrian galleys just to taunt them. She hates [[The Set]] for their use of chains and forced labor.

# Background
Before the occupation, she was a renowned pilot for merchant vessels. She lost her ship, the *Glinting Wave*, during the coup and now operates a small skiff out of [[Rekka]], ferrying messages to the insurgency.

In [[029 Preparing for the Ambush]], she coordinated the Reacher resistance for archer training under [[Mallow]], refining the aerial tactics of her scouts for the assault.

# Goals
- Sabotage the [[Bloodmarks]] fleet.
- Find a way to bypass the blockade to reach the Horneater Peaks.
- **Aerial Dominance:** Train her scouts to be an effective harassment force.

# Connections
- [[Uum'atu]]: Her leader.
- [[Cadence]]: A fellow Reacher she has heard rumors about.
- [[Mallow]]: Collaborated to train the Reacher archers.
