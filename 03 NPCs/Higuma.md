# Description
A brilliant Nurian artifabrian and the mother of [[Kuma]]. Though she is ethnically Nurian, she possesses the height and features that allow her to easily pass as an Alethi lighteyes.

# Appearance
Higuma has a regal bearing with dark, intelligent eyes and hair often kept in complex Alethi braids. 

# Personality
Meticulous and protective. She is a woman of science who values the safety of her family above her own groundbreaking research.

# Background
Higuma was the true mastermind behind the high-output [[Nurian Gems]] stabilization techniques required for [[The Experimental Steamship]]. Before the demonstration in [[Tomat]], she was abducted by the [[Bloodmarks]] to ensure the syndicate maintains a monopoly on her designs. She is currently being held in a secure facility in [[Bodenar]], forced to refine gemstones for [[Grand Justicar Malice]].

# Goals
- Ensure the safety of her son, [[Kuma]].
- Sabotage the Bloodmarks' efforts to weaponize her research.
- Escape her captivity in [[Bodenar]].

# Connections
- [[Kuma]]: Her son.
- [[Bloodmarks]]: Her captors.
- [[Grand Justicar Malice]]: The man overseeing her forced labor.
