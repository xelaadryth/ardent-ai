# Description
A cruel Veden lieutenant within the [[Bloodmarks]] who was killed by Painspren in [[Shadesmar]].

# History
Jalis was responsible for the raid on [[N'tal]]'s family. He was encountered in [[Shadesmar]] commanding the *Wandering Eye*. During [[027 Battle of Ships and Spren]], he was maimed by [[N'tal]] and subsequently swarmed by Painspren, who fed on his agony until he died.

# Connections
- [[Johb]]: His former superior.
- [[N'tal]]: The man who finally brought him to justice.
