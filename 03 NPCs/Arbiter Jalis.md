# Description
A cruel and theatrical Lieutenant within the [[Bloodmarks]] syndicate. He was stationed around [[Haka'alaku]], but was killed by Painspren in [[Shadesmar]].

# Appearance
A lean Veden man with sharp features and a mocking smile. He typically carries a grappling hook and a custom crossbow. Wears an odd key on a necklace.

# Personality
Smug and calculating. He views combat as a performance and prefers environmental sabotage over direct confrontation. He enjoys weaponizing the trauma of the enslaved spren.

# Background
Jalis was a key operative during the ambush of [[N'tal]]'s family. After escaping the party in [[Revolar]], he was reassigned to the Cognitive Realm to help secure the Horneater Perpendicularity.

He was encountered in [[Shadesmar]] commanding the *Wandering Eye*. During [[027 Battle of Ships and Spren]], he was maimed by [[N'tal]] and subsequently swarmed by Painspren, who fed on his agony until he died.

# Goals
- Serve the interests of the [[Bloodmarks]] in [[Haka'alaku]].
- Break [[N'tal]]'s spirit by forcing him to relive his past failures.

# Connections
- [[Johb]]: His former superior.
- [[Grand Justicar Malice]]: His ultimate commander.
- [[N'tal]]: The man who finally brought him to justice.
