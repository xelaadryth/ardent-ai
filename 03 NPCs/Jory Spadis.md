# Description
A  teenage boy secretly working for the [[Knights of Dusk]] and the son of [[General Spade]] who has recently begun hearing the whispers of a spren. He is an undercover agent working in the Shield Ring, pretending to be assisting a "father" who is an officer (Edis) in the city Guard. Several darkeyed sympathizers in the guard help enable him, not knowing it's not an official mission at all.

# Appearance
Around 15, with messy brown hair and bright green eyes that look trustingly at you.

# Personality
Usually happy and likes helping others. He is deeply traumatized by his capture by the [[Bloodmarks]]. Brave and willing to take risks.

# Background
The son of [[General Spade]] of the [[Knights of Dusk]] was captured after seen whispering to himself after helping out a darkeyed being bullied by other lighteyed by the [[Bloodmarks]], knowing there's a reward for his capture as a [[Possessed]]. He was captured by [[Arbiter Jalis]] during a transfer operation and held for interrogation regarding the rebel hideouts. He is a budding Windrunner, following in the footsteps of his future savior [[Beric Quinn]].

# Goals
- Survive his captivity.
- Impress his father.
- Understand the voices in his head.

# Connections
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: The organization that attempted to protect him.
- [[Arbiter Jalis]]: His captor.
