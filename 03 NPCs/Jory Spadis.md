# Description
A young teenage boy secretly working for the [[Knights of Dusk]] and the son of [[General Spade]] who has recently begun hearing the whispers of a spren. He is an undercover agent working in the Shield Ring.

# Appearance
Barely into his teenage years, with messy black hair and light eyes that constantly dart toward unseen shadows.

# Personality
Usually happy and likes helping others. He is deeply traumatized by his capture by the [[Bloodmarks]]. Brave and willing to take risks.

# Background
Recruited by the [[Knights of Dusk]] after he was discovered whispering to himself by the [[Bloodmarks]], knowing there's a reward for his capture as a [[Possessed]]. He was captured by [[Arbiter Jalis]] during a transfer operation and held for interrogation regarding the rebel hideouts. He is a budding Windrunner, following in the footsteps of his future savior [[Beric Quinn]].

# Goals
- Survive his captivity.
- Impress his father.
- Understand the voices in his head.
