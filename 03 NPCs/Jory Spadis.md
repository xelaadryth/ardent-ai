# Description
The teenage son of [[General Spade]] who has been successfully rescued from [[Truthkeepers]] custody.

# History
Captured by [[Bloodmarks]] and nearly maimed by [[Adjudicator Peton]] in a [[Slipmarket]] warehouse. He was rescued by the party during [[020 Warehouse Rescue]]. Following his rescue, he guided the party back to the [[Darkstone Forest]] base in [[022 Back to Base]], where he discovered a tracking device from [[Tash The Hound]] in his pocket.

# Connections
- [[Mallow]]: Rescued him from his chains and led him to safety.
- [[General Spade]]: His father and commanding officer.
