# Description
A lighteyed intelligence operative within the [[Bloodmarks]] who acts as the primary handler for high-tier syndicate assets. She is a worldhopper from Scadrial who operates with chilling efficiency and an unnerving ability to read those around her.

# Appearance
Rissa Vale is typically seen wearing a dark, high-collared coat of off-world tailoring. A thick, heavy ledger is permanently chained to her belt, which she references frequently. She carries herself with a stillness that is often mistaken for passivity, and she never raises her voice, forcing those who speak with her to lean in to listen.

# Personality
Observant, patient, and dangerously well-informed. Rissa possesses an unsettling knack for analyzing the motivations and fears of her adversaries. She treats conversation as a tactical exercise, using keen observations to undermine the confidence of her targets. She avoids direct confrontation, preferring to manipulate situations from the shadows.

# Background
Rissa is the commander of both [[Tash The Hound]] and [[Varo Three-Knives]], coordinating their retrieval and logistics operations in the [[Scarstone Quarter]]. She keeps the syndicate's complex trade ledgers and is known to be the only person who truly knows the extent of the Bloodmarks' operations in [[Revolar]].
