# Description
A lighteyed intelligence operative within the [[Bloodmarks]] who acts as the primary handler for high-tier syndicate assets. She is a worldhopper from Scadrial who operates with chilling efficiency and an unnerving ability to read those around her.

She's a twinborn copper ferring (storing memories in metalminds), and tin allomancer (letting her hear and see everything).

# Appearance
Rissa Vale is typically seen wearing a dark, high-collared coat of off-world tailoring, cut for practicality rather than fashion. Several slender copper rings adorn her fingers, accompanied by a pair of simple copper bracelets worn smooth from years of use. The jewelry is tasteful but oddly consistent; those who know her well cannot recall ever seeing her without it.

She carries herself with a stillness often mistaken for passivity. Her eyes rarely linger on a speaker's face, instead flicking briefly to hands, doorways, windows, and the surrounding room before returning to the conversation. Nothing about the habit feels obvious until someone realizes she seems to notice details others miss entirely.

Rissa never raises her voice, forcing those who speak with her to lean in to listen. She maintains unwavering eye contact when she chooses, yet somehow gives the unsettling impression that she is paying attention to everything else as well. People often leave conversations with her feeling heard, understood, and vaguely exposed.

# Personality
Observant, patient, and dangerously well-informed. Rissa possesses an unsettling knack for remembering the tiniest details which seem almost impossible to have noticed. She treats conversation as a tactical exercise, using keen observations to undermine the confidence of her targets. She avoids direct confrontation, preferring to manipulate situations from the shadows.

# Background
Rissa is the commander of both [[Tash The Hound]] and [[Varo Three-Knives]], coordinating their retrieval and logistics operations in the [[Scarstone Quarter]]. She keeps the syndicate's complex trade ledgers and is known to be the only person who truly knows the extent of the Bloodmarks' operations in [[Revolar]].

# Goals
- Manage Bloodmarks intelligence and logistics.
- Neutralize the influence of the [[Knights of Dusk]].
- Study the party and learn the extent of their knowledge.
