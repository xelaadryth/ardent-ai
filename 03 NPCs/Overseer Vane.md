# Description
A cold and calculating Scadrian Allomancer serving as the primary taskmaster in [[Haka'alaku]]. He is responsible for managing the labor of the enslaved [[Peakspren]].

# Personality
Vane is a man of absolute efficiency. He views the spren purely as biological machinery and holds a deep disdain for the "whimsical" nature of the Cognitive Realm. He is known for using Rioting to keep the slave gangs in a state of perpetual, directed anger, which he believes increases their rowing speed.

# Background
A low-ranking member of the [[Bloodmarks]] who saw the occupation of the perpendicularity as his chance to earn a title as a Suit. He personally oversaw the dismantling of the Haka'alaku Senate.

# Goals
- Ensure the quota of [[Nurian Gems]] and refined ores reaches the fleet on time.
- Capture the "terrorists" operating out of [[Rekton]].
