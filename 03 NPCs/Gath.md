# Description
The stoic and efficient First Mate of the [[Deepen Light]]. He handles the day-to-day logistics of the ship, allowing [[Varo Three-Knives]] to focus on social engineering and deal-making.

# Appearance
A large Veden man with a thick, square-cut beard and a perpetually furrowed brow. He has a prosthetic iron hook on his left hand, which he uses with surprising dexterity to adjust rigging.

# Personality
Gath is a man of few words who values order and competence. He is fiercely loyal to Varo, having been saved by the Captain from a Veden debtor's prison. He tolerates the [[Bloodmarks]] only because they provide steady work.

# Background
Formerly a legitimate merchant sailor, Gath was framed for theft by a rival company. Varo broke him out of prison and gave him a place on the [[Deepen Light]]. Gath knows the ship's secret compartments better than anyone.

# Goals
- Keep the [[Deepen Light]] running at peak efficiency.
- Protect Varo from his own overly-friendly nature.

# Connections
- [[Varo Three-Knives]]: Captain and savior.
- [[Deepen Light]]: His home and responsibility.
