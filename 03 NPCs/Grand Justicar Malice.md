# Description
Temeret Malice is a high-ranking Scadrian worldhopper and the primary commander of the [[Bloodmarks]] worldhopper fleet. She is a formidable Twinborn who utilizes the Metallic Arts to dominate the trade routes of [[Shadesmar]].

# Appearance
Possesses Scadrian features (resembling Northern Rosharans) with red hair and cold, lightgray eyes. She wears high-collared, multi-pocketed Veden robes designed for carrying various metal vials and feruchemical bracers.

# Personality
Malice is the embodiment of "the law as a weapon." She views the Cosmere as a series of markets to be conquered. She is a clinical strategist who views the Rosharan Radiants as inefficient variables to be neutralized.

# Background
Malice is not a native of Roshar. She hails from Scadrial and serves as the bridge between the syndicate's Rosharan operations and its interplanetary interests. She orchestrated the takeover of [[N'tal]]'s family mines to secure a steady supply of investiture-reactive gems for export.

# Twinborn Abilities
Allomantic and Feruchemical Chromium (Leecher and Spinner) compounder.

# Secrets
Malice is currently searching for a way to use [[Nurian Gems]] as a storage medium for longer term Stormlight storage offworld.
