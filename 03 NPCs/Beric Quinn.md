# Description
A Nurian hunter and [[Knights of Dusk]] spy who is currently recovering from severe wounds sustained while warning the insurgency of the [[Truthkeepers]]' assault.

# Background
In [[024 To Kill a Shardbearer]], a wounded Beric returned to the [[Darkstone Forest]] base to warn [[General Spade]] that the march had begun and that his status as a spy had been compromised by the [[Truthkeepers]].
