# Description
A bartender in the [[Haka'alaku]] camp, colloquially known as "Fifth Finger Curls to Complete the Fist Punching You in the Face". He is a former soldier turned craftsman by necessity.

# Personality
Informative but disillusioned by the state of the camp. He explains the Unkalaki societal structure to the party.

# Background
As a fifth son, he fills the role of a craftsman/bartender. He notes the poor food quality and shoddy construction of the base, stemming from the fact that most Unkalaki there are soldiers, not builders or farmers.

# Goals
- Maintain order and provide some semblance of comfort to the laborers.
