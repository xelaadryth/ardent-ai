# Description
A world-weary Thaylen ex-pirate and former [[Bloodmarks]] enforcer who defected to the party during [[The Darkstone Siege]]. Though he spent years convinced that his destiny was crime, he has chosen to turn over a new leaf after the sacrifice of his closest friends. He possesses an unusual, intense loyalty to [[Fina]], crediting her with his humanity simply because she remembered his name.

# Appearance
Gorlo is a tall, wiry Thaylen man with the characteristic long, white eyebrows of his people, which he habitually tucks behind his ears when preparing for work. He wears an old, faded, salt-weary leather vest over patched and roughly-tanned leather armor. He has a copper hoop in his left ear and faded, indigo-ink tattoos of a many-legged khornak down his right forearm—a relic of his adventurous youth pirating Longbrow's Straits. His most identifying characteristic is a prominent gap between his crooked teeth that becomes apparent whenever he speaks.

# Personality
Gorlo is streetsmart and perceptive, possessing a sharp wit sharpened by years of surviving brutal ship captains and syndicate politics. While he is physically strong and quick, he harbors a deep-seated aversion to using his knives for violence, preferring intimidation and roughing up targets over lethal combat. He is a mirror image of the party's own journey; a man who turned his back on a dark organization despite his complicity in its history, seeking a new path among those who showed him mercy.

# Background
As a lad, Gorlo joined a Thaylen pirate crew for the promise of adventure. That illusion was shattered during a disastrous raid where his best friend was killed and the crew decimated. Under a new, more brutal captain, Gorlo became complicit in heinous crimes, eventually coming to believe he was beyond redemption. He eventually escaped and joined the [[Bloodmarks]], where he found a slightly less evil niche as a "Lash" focused on escort and delivery rather than direct slaughter.

During his time in the syndicate, he looked after **Piggy**, a younger, frightened member whom Gorlo treated as a little brother. He also worked alongside **Fang**, a man who tried to be good but struggled with fits of uncontrollable rage. During [[The Darkstone Siege]], Piggy and Fang were killed while heroically stalling [[Tash The Hound]] to allow the party to escape. He holds a lot of survivor's guilt since he knew the first to run would distract Tash, and he froze in indecisiveness rather than consciously trying to sacrifice his friends, but the end result was the same. Finding himself the sole survivor of his trio, Gorlo surrendered to the party for judgment and was spared.

# Goals
- Protect [[Fina]] and the party to repay the mercy shown to him.
- Atone for his years of piracy and syndicate violence.
- Overcome survivor's guilt and forgive himself.
