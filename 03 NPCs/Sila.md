# Description
A large, imposing darkeyed man who looks even more dangerous than he is. While he presents as a simple brute, he is a survivor who has been recruited as the second-in-command for [[Koloko]]'s rising Ghostblood cell.

# Appearance
Sila is marked by numerous battle scars, most notably a jagged diagonal gash that crosses his lips. He carries a massive, well-used axe and typically smells of smoke and cheap ale.

# Personality
Sila is craftier than he looks. While he likes drink and can be impulsive with his coin, he has learned to control his emotional outbursts to better serve as a professional enforcer. He provides a tactical, grounded balance to [[Koloko]]'s more elaborate schemes.

# Background
Originally a low-tier darkeyes at the Truthkeeper camp, Sila was liberated by [[Chip]] and [[Karani]] after faking his death. Upon reaching [[Revolar]], he was vetted by [[Koloko]], who recognized that the big man's brutish exterior hid a cunning mind. Sila now serves as the primary muscle and lieutenant for Koloko's operations.

# Goals
- **Survival:** Avoid the notice of the Truthkeepers, who believe him to be dead.
- **Loyalty:** Help [[Koloko]] establish his business, finding steady work more reliable than freelance thuggery.

# Secrets
Sila's "death" by fire was meticulously staged. If any Truthkeepers, particularly [[Adjudicator Peton]], were to recognize him in [[Revolar]], his ruse would be discovered.

# Connections
- [[Koloko]]: His commander and the "mastermind" he supports.
- [[Chip]]: One of his saviors and a current contact within the Ghostbloods.
- [[Karani]]: The other half of the team that freed him from hard labor.
