# Description
An ambitious and "learned" darkeyed Witness who is desperately attempting to climb the hierarchy of the [[Truthkeepers]]. He is known for being a "tryhard" who over-performs his duties to attract the attention of his superiors and attaches himself to successful units.

# Appearance
Neval is a man of average build with sharp, eager features. He is meticulously groomed, his Witness robes perpetually pressed and devoid of a single speck of dust. Instead of a quill, he often carries a set of ivory 'priority markers'—items traditionally used by high-ranking officers to denote urgent logistics—and a small, silver whistle used to signal for subordinates.

# Personality
Greedy for rank and social validation. Neval is a consummate sycophant, frequently seen lingering near [[Adjudicator Peton]] or attempting to mirror the stoic professionalism of [[High Enforcer Aylen]]. He views the party's rapid promotion to Enforcer with intense envy but masks it with oily flattery, constantly offering 'unsolicited reports' and logistical assistance to stay within their orbit.

# Background
Neval was part of the original recruitment wagons at the [[Truthkeeper Camp]]. He quickly distinguished himself through an uncanny ability to memorize and parrot back Truthkeeper protocols and legal precedents, making himself useful to his superiors. He believes that by attaching himself to 'rising stars' like the party, he can ride their momentum to his own promotion.

# Goals
- Earn a promotion to the rank of Enforcer.
- Secure a personal commendation from [[Adjudicator Peton]].
- Amass enough wealth and status to be treated as an equal by the lighteyes within the organization.

# Hooks
- **Sycophantic Intelligence:** Neval may provide the party with more information than necessary if he believes it will make him look competent or if he thinks the party has the influence to reward him.
- **The Mental Ledger:** He keeps meticulous mental notes on every operation, which could be a treasure trove of intelligence if extracted through persuasion or threat.

# Connections
- [[Adjudicator Peton]]: The officer he is constantly trying to impress.
- [[Senny]]: A fellow Witness whom he views as his primary rival for promotion.
- [[Cazo]] and [[Hine]]: Others in his squad
- [[Tahs]]: An initiate he occasionally collaborates with to gain social leverage.
