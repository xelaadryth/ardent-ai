# Description
An ambitious and "learned" darkeyed Witness who is desperately attempting to climb the hierarchy of the [[Truthkeepers]]. He is known for being a "tryhard" who over-performs his duties to attract the attention of his superiors.

# Appearance
Neval is a man of average build with sharp, eager features and dark brown eyes. He is rarely seen without a ledger or a reporting quill in hand. He keeps his Witness robes meticulously clean, almost to the point of obsession, to separate himself from the "common" darkeyes.

# Personality
Greedy for rank and social validation. Neval is highly efficient but lacks the spiritual depth of peers like [[Senny]]. He views the [[Truthkeepers]] primarily as a vehicle for his own advancement and treats every assignment as an opportunity to prove his superiority over other darkeyes.

# Background
Neval was part of the original recruitment wagons at the [[Truthkeeper Camp]], where he quickly distinguished himself through his literacy and administrative competence. He believes that by showing absolute loyalty and clinical efficiency, he can bypass the traditional Alethi social barriers and eventually earn the rank of Enforcer.

# Goals
- Earn a promotion to the rank of Enforcer.
- Secure a personal commendation from [[Adjudicator Peton]].
- Amass enough wealth and status to be treated as an equal by the lighteyes within the organization.

# Hooks
- **Over-Eager Reporting:** Neval may provide the party with more information than necessary if he believes it will help him look more competent in a report.
- **The Paper Trail:** He keeps meticulous notes on every operation, which could be a treasure trove of intelligence if stolen or intercepted.

# Connections
- [[Adjudicator Peton]]: The officer he is constantly trying to impress.
- [[Senny]]: A fellow Witness whom he views as his primary rival for promotion.
- [[Tahs]]: An initiate he occasionally collaborates with to gain social leverage.
