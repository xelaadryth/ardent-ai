# Description
The cunning and duplicitous former advisor to Brightlord [[Amaram]]. He is a high-ranking member of the [[Sons of Honor]] who exploited his position to further the society's radical agenda.

# Appearance
A middle-aged Alethi man with sharp, calculating light blue eyes and a perpetually smooth, reassuring voice. He typically wears the understated but fine silks of a high-level administrative official.

# Personality
Ambitious and fanatical. Beneath a facade of administrative efficiency lies a man driven by the belief that the Heralds must be forced to return at any cost. He views people, including his former employer, as expendable tools.

# Background
While serving [[Amaram]] in [[Tomat]], Ridius secretly managed the recruitment drives to identify potential "fuel" for the coming conflict. After [[Helaran]] assassinated [[Amaram]], Merim stepped into the power vacuum. He held a private ceremony supposedly in [[Tien]]'s honor for slaying the assassin but used the occasion to claim the Shardblade and Shardplate won by the boy. He has since traveled to the [[Shattered Plains]] in Amaram's stead to fulfill the original mission of the [[Sons of Honor]].

# Goals
- **Restoration:** Induce the return of the Heralds by creating a world of conflict.
- **Assumption of Power:** Utilize [[Amaram]]'s stolen Shards and reputation to influence the War of Reckoning.

# Connections
- [[Amaram]]: His late employer.
- [[Tien]]: The soldier he robbed of his rightful Shards.
- [[Sons of Honor]]: The secret society he serves.
