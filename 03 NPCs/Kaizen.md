# Description
A Malwish Mistborn and a Suit of [[The Set]]. He claims to be the "Hero of Ages" and is currently a stowaway/captive of the party in [[Shadesmar]].

# Personality
Spoiled and melodramatic. He ran away from the [[Ghostbloods]] because [[The Set]] treated him with the "respect" he believes his destiny deserves. He frequently chitchats with [[Iiko]] and displays his Allomancy to impress the party.

# History
Found hiding on the [[Deepen Light]] after the transition to [[Shadesmar]]. He outranks [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]] within [[The Set]] hierarchy. He participated in the skirmish against the *Wandering Eye* and survived an Angerspren attack.

# Goals
- Acquire a Shardblade.
- Prove himself as the true [[Hero of Ages]].

# Connections
- [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]]: His subordinate.
- [[Johb]]: A fellow Suit within the organization.
