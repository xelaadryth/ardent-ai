# Description
A Malwish Iron Misting (Iron Puller) and Suit of [[The Set]] serving as the Overseer of [[Haka'alaku]]. He is responsible for guarding [[Mother's Gate]] and managing the flow of [[Nurian Gems]].

# Background
Johb is a clinical taskmaster who uses Hemalurgic rioting to control his slave gangs. Intelligence from [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]] confirms he is a Lurcher, capable of pulling on metals with Allomancy.
