# Description
A Skybreaker squire under [[Istrum]], sent to observe the party. He seeks to deliver judgment to the [[Bloodmarks]], viewing their crimes as more significant than those of the party, and sees the squad as his best chance to achieve this.

# Appearance

# Personality

# Background
Introduced to the party by [[Uum'atu]] in [[Rekka]], [[Atlas]] joined them on their infiltration of [[Haka'alaku]]. He views his mission as technically complying with [[High Enforcer Aylen]]'s instructions to watch the party, but with the intent of subverting them to a higher purpose: bringing justice to the [[Bloodmarks]].

# Goals
- Deliver judgment to the [[Bloodmarks]] for their crimes.
- Monitor the party while steering them towards acts of justice.

# Connections
