# Description
An Unkalaki laborer working at the "New Consortium" hub in [[Haka'alaku]]. He is a fourth-son, formerly a soldier, now forced into labor. His real name is Dal'ankala'hiludina, which means "Soul Returns at Daybreak".

# Personality
Reverent, confused, and anxious for the safety of his family. He treats the party with deep respect, believing them to be elite Bloodmarks/Set agents.

# Background
Along with his kin, he was taken to [[Bodenar]] and then forced to labor in [[Haka'alaku]] under [[Johb]]. He is trying to be helpful to earn trust and find information about his family's whereabouts.

# Goals
- Secure the safety of his family.
- Survive the harsh labor conditions of the occupation.

# Connections
- [[Johb]]: Overseer of the camp.
- [[Haka'alaku]]: His current location.
