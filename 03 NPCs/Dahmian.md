# Description
A tall, restless Nurian mercenary and culinary enthusiast who serves as a Truthkeeper Enforcer while secretly spying for the [[Knights of Dusk]].

# Appearance
Standing at 6'0" with olive skin, Dahmian has deep dark brown eyes and thick black hair. He possesses the lean, athletic build of a stunt fighter and carries himself with an ease that suggests he is always ready to move on.

# Personality
Dahmian is a wandering soul who views the world through a lens of flavor and freedom. He is a mercenary foodie who is charming but perpetually detached, driven by a deep-seated fear of being trapped. He avoids long-term commitments, viewing them as "golden handcuffs" that restrict his choices. He only joins causes that offer him a clear exit or a chance to sample local delicacies.

# Background
Dahmian’s childhood was defined by starvation, a trauma that manifested as an adult obsession with tasting every kind of food Roshar has to offer. He has spent his life alternating between performing stunt fighting in acting troupes and taking actual mercenary contracts. 

He arrived at the [[Truthkeeper Camp]] in a wagon alongside [[Karani]] and [[Fina]], joining the organization in a later wave of Nurian recruits. While stationed in [[Salimset]], he single-handedly captured a [[Possessed]] individual and brought them in for judgment. This display of efficiency quickly elevated him to the rank of Enforcer under [[Adjudicator Peton]]. Upon his transfer to [[Revolar]], he teamed up with [[Beric Quinn]].

He is currently acting as an informant for the [[Knights of Dusk]]. His motivation for spying is less about morality and more about ensuring he is not trapped by the rigid hierarchies of the [[Truthkeepers]].

# Goals
- Taste all varieties of food and wine across the princedoms during his travels.
- Avoid any situation or allegiance that makes him feel trapped or restricted.
- Maintain his cover within the [[Truthkeepers]] while securing an exit strategy via the rebellion.

# Hooks
- **Restless Ally:** During high-stakes events like [[The Darkstone Siege]], Dahmian may be the first to abandon the [[Knights of Dusk]] if he perceives the situation as a cage with no escape.
- **Weapon Taboo:** Though his preferred weapon is a rapier, he adheres to Alethi martial taboos by wielding a shortspear and knife in public.
