# Description
A massive and battered Peakspren who served as a rowing slave before being liberated. He is an architect by trade and has now been trained in naval artillery.

# Personality
Once a respected architect, his spirit is bruised but not broken. He is stoic and enduring, serving as the leader of the liberated oar-crew. 

# Background
He refused to cooperate with [[Johb]]'s demands for new fortifications, leading to his reassignment to the oars. He was liberated by [[Galeth-son-Thald]] during the seizure of the *Wandering Eye*.

In [[029 Three Ship Ambush]], Vun'akal led his crew through rigorous training under [[Galeth-son-Thald]], learning to operate the ship's cannons for the upcoming assault on [[Haka'alaku]].

# Goals
- Keep the other slaves from losing hope.
- Sabotage the galley from the inside when the time is right.
- **Siege Mastery:** Master the artillery of the *Wandering Eye* to reclaim his home.

# Connections
- [[Johb]]: His tormentor.
- [[Uum'atu]]: His friend who escaped.
- [[Galeth-son-Thald]]: His liberator and instructor in naval gunnery.
