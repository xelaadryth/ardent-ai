# Description
A semi-retired Veden bounty hunter who has seen too much death. He operates with a cold, professional efficiency, though his heart is no longer in the hunt.

# Personality
Deeply pessimistic and nihilistic. Marcus has given up on his own values and views the world with a bleak fatalism. He tells himself that by killing his targets, he is saving them from the harshness of a lonely life like his own. He considers death by a [[Shardblade]] to be a clean and merciful end.

# Background
Once a man who hunted for the thrill and the challenge of facing famous warriors, Marcus has aged into a man who sees killing as the only thing he is good for. He is unaffiliated with any group, but he has been drawn back into the trade by the significant bounty placed on the party by the [[Bloodmarks]] following the events of [[Saving Jory at the Abandoned Docks]].

# Goals
- Collect the bounty on the party.
- Find a "clean" end to his own career.

# Connections
- [[Bloodmarks]]: His current employers via bounty contract.
- [[Jory Spadis]]: Linked to the party's rescue efforts.
