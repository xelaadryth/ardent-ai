# Description
A low-tier lighteyed Witness within the [[Truthkeepers]]. Following his encounter with [[Valerius Atol]] and the revelation of the organization's atrocities, his faith in his mission has been shattered.

# Appearance
Senny has a face that seems permanently set in a serious, matter-of-fact frown. He wears the clean, precise robes of a Truthkeeper Witness.

# Personality
Formerly unyielding and fanatical, he is currently in shock after realizing the [[Truthkeepers]] have been deceiving him regarding the nature of the [[Possessed]] and the history of the world.

# Background
He joined the [[Truthkeepers]] to protect Roshar. After seeing the illusions projected by [[Iiko]] confirming the atrocities, he is currently captured by the [[Knights of Dusk]].

# Goals
- Process the betrayal of his organization and the survival of his friend [[Valerius Atol]].

# Connections
- [[Valerius Atol]]: His friend whom he believed dead.
- [[High Witness Yeshan]]: The spiritual figurehead he previously served with fanatical devotion.
- [[Neval]]: A peer in his squad
