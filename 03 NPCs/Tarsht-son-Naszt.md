# Description
A Shin soldier and former unit-mate of [[Galeth-son-Thald]]. He currently serves as a guard for [[Johb]] in [[Haka'alaku]]. He led the defense against the party's infiltration in [[032 The New Consortium]].

# Personality
Rigid, defensive, and deeply conditioned by the corrupt military hierarchy he served in. He views the chain of command as the ultimate authority, even when that authority is inherently corrupt.

# Background
Served in a specialized Shin military unit alongside [[Galeth-son-Thald]]. The unit was deeply corrupt, frequently taking secret orders from a foreign [[Bloodmarks]] merchant to sabotage business competition. Their commander, [[Commander Vahn-son-Midal]], disguised these raids as legitimate military maneuvers. During a raid on a supply caravan, [[Commander Vahn]] ordered the killing of a merchant family to eliminate witnesses. When [[Galeth-son-Thald]] refused the order, a fellow soldier named [[Kesh-son-Zemit]] executed the family. Tarscht-son-Naszt was later told that [[Galeth-son-Thald]] was marked for execution for his disobedience, though Galeth had already fled into exile by then. After the unit was exposed for burning an innocent Azish village, many of the soldiers, including Tarscht-son-Naszt and [[Commander Vahn-son-Midal]], fought their way out and defected to the [[Bloodmarks]] merchant they previously served. In [[032 The New Consortium]], he was identified by [[Galeth-son-Thald]] while defending [[Haka'alaku]] against the party's infiltration.

# Goals
- Protect the interests of the Bloodmark merchant.
- Maintain his standing within the unit.
- Keep tabs on the movements of the Assassin in White, who has been linked to Shinovar.

# Connections

- [[Kensht]]: The soldier who carried out the executions in [[Galeth-son-Thald]]'s stead.
