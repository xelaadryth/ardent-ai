# Description
Valerius Dridius is a lighteyed defector from the [[Truthkeepers]], an old friend of [[Senny]], and currently a logistics leader within the [[Knights of Dusk]]. He carries the heavy burden of being a survivor of the purge of [[Talinan]].

# Personality
Cynical, haunted, but deeply principled. He balances his disillusionment with the [[Truthkeepers]] with a fierce commitment to the rebels he helped hide after the razing of Talinan. He remains conflicted about his friendship with [[Senny]], whom he sees as a victim of Truthkeeper propaganda.

# Background
Valerius was a low-ranking officer in the [[Truthkeepers]] who witnessed the razing of Talinan. While his superiors claimed it was a purge of the [[Possessed]], he saw the slaughter of innocents. He chose to help evacuate surviving families, including the parents of [[Dillind]], before defecting to the [[Knights of Dusk]]. He has since operated in the shadows of [[Revolar]].

# Connections
- [[Senny]]: His close friend from his time in the Truthkeepers, who believes Valerius died in Talinan.
- [[Dillind]]: One of the children he helped evacuate.
- [[General Spade]]: His commanding officer in the insurgency.
- [[Arbiter Jalis]]: A rival within the city he actively avoids.
