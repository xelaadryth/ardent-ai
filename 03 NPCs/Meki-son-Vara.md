# Description
A former Shin soldier and the primary catalyst for [[Galeth-son-Thald]]'s exile. Unlike Galeth, Meki does not seek penance; he has embraced the "freedom" of the outside world's violence.

# Appearance
A Shin man with a wide, flat face and cold, obsidian-colored eyes. He has discarded Shin robes for jagged Alethi-style leather and furs.

# Personality
Cynical and aggressive. He views Shin pacifism as a prison and Galeth's former leadership as "weakness disguised as virtue." He is proud of the act that got him exiled, viewing the killing as his first step toward true freedom.

# Background
Meki served under Galeth in Shinovar. During a routine patrol, he murdered an innocent girl (the daughter of [[Trel-son-Trel]]) after she allegedly "dishonored" the stone they stood upon. When Galeth exiled him, Meki fled east and found a home among the [[Bloodmarks]], using his training to become a high-tier enforcer. He is currently overseeing gemstone transfers in [[Bodenar]].

# Goals
- **Prove Superiority:** Show Galeth that the way of the warrior is the only way to survive.
- **Profit:** Accumulate enough wealth to one day return to Shinovar and "purify" it with fire.

# Connections
- [[Galeth-son-Thald]]: His former commander and current obsession.
- [[Grand Justicar Malice]]: His employer in [[Bodenar]].
