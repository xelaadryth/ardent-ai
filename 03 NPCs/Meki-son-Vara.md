# Description
A former Shin soldier and the primary catalyst for [[Galeth-son-Thald]]'s exile. Unlike Galeth, Meki does not seek penance; he has embraced the "freedom" of the outside world's violence.

# Appearance
A Shin man with a wide, flat face and cold, obsidian-colored eyes. He has discarded Shin robes for jagged Alethi-style leather and furs.

# Personality
Cynical and aggressive. He views Shin pacifism as a prison and Galeth's former leadership as "weakness disguised as virtue." He is proud of the act that got him exiled, viewing the killing as his first step toward true freedom. Strength justifies any action and he'll prove he's right by killing Galeth.

# Background
Meki served under Galeth in Shinovar. During a routine patrol, he murdered an innocent girl (the daughter of [[Tezsh-son-Rillan]]) after she allegedly "dishonored" the stone they stood upon. When Galeth exiled him, Meki fled east. He didn't know where to go originally and followed Galeth for awhile, but eventually found a home among the [[Bloodmarks]], using his training to become an Arbiter, and enjoyed that he was treated better than other soldiers in Shinovar. He is currently overseeing gemstone transfers in [[Bodenar]].

# Goals
- **Prove Superiority:** Show Galeth and Shinovar that the way of the killing is right, and peace is wrong and foolish.
- **Profit:** Accumulate enough wealth to one day return to Shinovar and "purify" it with fire.

# Connections
- [[Galeth-son-Thald]]: His former commander and current obsession.
- [[Grand Justicar Malice]]: His employer in [[Bodenar]].
