# Description
An arrogant and impeccably dressed Alethi noble from the [[Skyglass District]]. He is the head of a house currently seeking to absorb the Palir family's assets through a marriage contract.

# Appearance
Always seen in the latest high-noble fashions, usually in the colors of the [[Vamah Princedom]]. He carries himself with a sneering sense of entitlement.

# Personality
Cruel, calculating, and obsessed with status. He views marriage as a legal transaction and has no empathy for personal choice or grief. He is a predator who uses his social standing to bully those he considers inferior.

# Background
Juran paid a significant bride-price to Renna’s father during a time of financial crisis for House Palir. When [[Renna Palir]] fled to the [[Truthkeepers]], he viewed it as a theft of his property rather than a pursuit of freedom. He currently operates within the high circles of [[Revolar]].

# Goals
- Force [[Renna Palir]] to fulfill the betrothal contract.
- Secure the signatures of Renna's father to finalize the merger of their houses.
- Maintain his reputation in the [[Skyglass District]].

# Connections
- [[Renna Palir]]: His intended bride who escaped.
- [[Fina]]: An outlander noble who publicly challenged him.
- [[Highlord Paladar]]: A social and political peer.
