# Description
A calm and level-headed Ardent of the Devotary of Insight who operates as a deep-cover spy for the [[Truthkeepers]]. While she presents as a scholar seeking to preserve religious purity, she is actively infiltrating the [[Knights of Dusk]] to monitor their efforts to revive the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]].

# Appearance
Surusa wears the humble, practical grey robes of a scholar-ardent, typically carrying a heavy satchel for books and parchment. Her movements are precise and deliberate, and she maintains an air of academic detachment that masks her true purpose.

# Personality
She is deeply fundamentalist and adheres strictly to ancient religious texts. To Surusa, the original [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]] were traitors of the highest order, and any attempt to revive them is a blasphemy that threatens the soul of Roshar. She remains chillingly calm, even in high-stress situations.

# Background
Surusa is the cousin of [[Lila]]. She accepted an invitation to the [[Knights of Dusk]] to act as a mole. She uses her access to the [[Scholar's Rest]] to identify and "recover" (steal) dangerous texts, delivering them instead to the [[Truthkeepers]]. 

During the [[The Traitor Investigation]], she was revealed to be the source of the leaks that led to the compromise of the [[Fieldwards]] and [[Stackhouses]] safehouses.

# Goals
- **Espionage:** Monitor [[General Spade]] and [[Khria]] for signs of budding Radiance.
- **Religious Purification:** Systematically remove or destroy texts favoring the Radiants.
- **The Silent Oath:** Identify high-priority targets for [[High Enforcer Aylen]].

# Evidence of Betrayal
- **Spanreed:** Carries a concealed spanreed and paper for rapid reporting to the [[Vamah Archivum]].
- **Glyphward Scraps:** Keeps woman-script communication logs in her glyphward brazier at the [[Devotary of Insight]].
- **Document Theft:** Frequently removes mythological texts from [[Scholar's Rest]] to keep them out of rebel hands.

# Connections
- [[Lila]]: Her cousin and unintentional facilitator.
- [[Truthkeepers]]: Her true organization.
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: The organization she has betrayed.
