# Description
A calm and level-headed Ardent of the Devotary of Insight who operates as a deep-cover spy for the [[Truthkeepers]]. While she presents as a scholar seeking to preserve religious purity, she is actively infiltrating the [[Knights of Dusk]] to monitor their efforts to revive the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]].

# Appearance
Surusa wears the humble, practical grey robes of a scholar-ardent, typically carrying a heavy satchel for books and parchment. Her movements are precise and deliberate, and she maintains an air of academic detachment that masks her true purpose.

# Personality
She is deeply fundamentalist and adheres strictly to ancient religious texts. To Surusa, the original [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]] were traitors of the highest order, and any attempt to revive them is a blasphemy that threatens the soul of Roshar. She remains chillingly calm, even in high-stress situations, viewing her espionage as a sacred duty to protect the world from a return of the Desolations.

# Background
Surusa is the cousin of [[Lila]], a laundry worker in the [[Slipmarket]]. When [[Lila]]—who mistakenly believes Surusa to be a vulnerable scribe in need of protection—invited her to join the [[Knights of Dusk]], Surusa accepted immediately. She uses her position within the insurgency and her access to the [[Scholar's Rest]] to identify and "recover" (steal) dangerous texts that might aid the rebels, delivering them instead to the [[Truthkeepers]].

# Goals
- **Espionage:** Monitor [[General Spade]] and [[Khria]] for signs of budding Radiance.
- **Religious Purification:** Systematically remove or destroy any texts that paint the ancient Radiants in a positive light.
- **The Silent Oath:** Prove her value to [[High Enforcer Aylen]] by identifying high-priority targets for "neutralization."
- **Access the Heart:** Use the events of [[The Fieldwards Purge]] to be taken to the rebellion's main base of operations.

# Events
- [[The Fieldwards Purge]]: Orchestrated a "failed" raid to gain the trust of surviving rebel leaders.

# Hooks
- **The Missing Scribe:** [[Lila]] may ask the party to escort her cousin to a safehouse, unaware that Surusa is mapping the route for a [[Truthkeepers]] raid.
- **The Censored Book:** A rebel researcher notes that several rare historical scrolls have vanished from a safehouse, all of which were last seen in Surusa's care.

# Connections
- [[Lila]]: Her cousin, whom she is manipulating for access to the rebellion.
- [[Truthkeepers]]: Her true organization.
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: The organization she is currently infiltrating.
