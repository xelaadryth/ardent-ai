# Description
A calm and level-headed Ardent of the Devotary of Insight who operated as a deep-cover spy for the [[Truthkeepers]]. She is now identified and captured as the traitor within the [[Knights of Dusk]].

# Appearance
Surusa wears the humble, practical grey robes of a scholar-ardent. Her movements are precise and deliberate.

# Personality
Chillingly calm, even in high-stress situations. She remains a fundamentalist who believes the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]] are traitors and blasphemers.

# Background
Cousin of [[Lila]]. She infiltrated the [[Knights of Dusk]] to monitor their efforts to revive the Radiants. She was unmasked by the party and [[Valerius Atol]] after investigations revealed her secret spanreed and communications with the [[Vamah Archivum]].

# Goals
- Formerly: Espionage and removal of pro-Radiant texts.
- Current: Imprisonment by the insurgency.

# Connections
- [[Lila]]: Her cousin.
- [[Truthkeepers]]: Her true organization.
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: The organization she betrayed.
