The '03 Stormblessed Arc' has been marked as inactive and superseded by the new '03 Shadesmar Arc'. This new arc focuses on the party's transition into the Cognitive Realm during the climax of the Darkstone Siege. I have introduced Scadrian Mechanics and a worldhopper branch of the Bloodmarks, led by the Twinborn Temeret Malice, to provide a unique cross-Cosmere threat. Zabriel now serves as the accidental provider of the power source (stolen Nurian Gems) that enabled Kuma's Transportation feat.
