I've rethemed the Shadesmar encounter from a single large serpent to a swarm of Anger and Fear spren. In the Cognitive Realm, these minor spren manifest as tangible threats. I've created separate Tier 1 Minion statblocks for them so you can throw several at the party during the 'The Bead-Sea Ambush' event, reflecting the chaotic pressure of being surrounded in the glass-bead sea.
