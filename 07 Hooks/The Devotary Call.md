# Description
[[Ardent Kaelis]] pointedly leaves open a book with his own handwritten undertext detailing the 'Truth of what happened' to track down who has been removing books and why. He sees potential in [[Iiko]] and wishes to recruit them into the [[Vorin Devotaries|Devotary of Sincerity]] to ground their manic pursuit of truth through historical and geological discipline. He has heard of the rumored 11th city but doubts it is real.
