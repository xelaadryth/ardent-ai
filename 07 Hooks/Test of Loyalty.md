# Description
When the party runs into [[Beric Quinn]] with [[Renna Palir]] not present, he casually lets them know that there's gonna be a sting strike on the [[Stackhouses]] in a specific block in a few days, that he personally tracked down a wounded Knights of Dusk undercover operative there following their trail of blood

If they pick up the glyph note from the [[Gemheart Inn]]: Get us intel from the [[The Vamah Archivum]] of next raid locations, report back at our last meeting location. Destroy this note.

Once they arrive at the [[Knights of Dusk]] safehouse, the party is told their assigned mission is to create a distraction on the east end of the [[Shield Ring]] and draw soldiers there, that there will be a Knights of Dusk hit on the south side at that exact time on 1172.2.4.5.

[[Beric Quinn]] is secretly a member of the [[Knights of Dusk]] in league with [[Renna Palir]]. They want to see what the party will do with information. Secretly the hit will be on the northwest side, by the Fieldwards, at [[1172-02-18 The Missing Supplies]].
