# Overview
The Cognitive Realm of Roshar, characterized by a vast sea of glass beads representing the ideas of physical objects and a dark sky with a stationary sun and multiple moons.

# Notable Features
- **The Bead Sea:** An ocean where solid land in the Physical Realm is replaced by depths of glass beads.
- **Spren Cities:** Urban centers inhabited by various types of sapient spren.
- **The Horneater Perpendicularity:** A rare point of transition between the Cognitive and Physical Realms. It is the site of the city [[Haka'alaku]].
- **[[Haka'alaku]]:** A major city of seven islands at the perpendicularity, currently occupied and blockaded by [[The Set]] under the oversight of [[Johb]].
- **[[Rekton]]:** A refugee trade town populated by those fleeing the occupation, led by [[Uum'atu]].

# Factions
- [[Bloodmarks]]: Operating a worldhopper pirate fleet led by [[Grand Justicar Malice]].
- [[Ghostbloods]]: Maintaining trade outposts and clandestine logistics.
