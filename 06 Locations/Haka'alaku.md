# Overview
A vibrant but currently oppressed city in [[Shadesmar]] located where [[Cultivation's Perpendicularity]] (the Horneater Perpendicularity) manifests in the Cognitive Realm. It is built upon seven floating islands of obsidian, interconnected by a complex web of wooden platforms and shanties.

# History
Historically, Haka'alaku was a bastion of inclusivity and democracy, ruled by a senate that included [[Peakspren]], [[Lightspren]], and even several humans from the Physical Realm. Citizenship was granted to any who resided within the city for a full cycle of moons. This era of peace ended when the [[Bloodmarks]], acting for [[The Set]], staged a violent coup to control the flow of goods and Investiture through the perpendicularity.

# Notable Features
- **The Seven Isles:** Clusters of glass land surrounded by bead seas where the city's shanties are most dense.
- **The Gilded Platforms:** A network of expensive, soulcast glasswood walkways that the occupation forces use to move between islands, often charging residents for passage.
- **The Slave Docks:** The deepest part of the harbor where [[Peakspren]] are chained to the oars of Scadrian-style galleys.

# Inhabitants
- [[Overseer Vane]]: The brutal commandant representing [[The Set]].
- [[Rusted Iron]]: A legendary peakspren architect turned rowing slave.

# Secrets
The old Senate chambers beneath the central island still contain a working spanreed connected to [[Rekton]], used by the resistance to coordinate escape attempts.
