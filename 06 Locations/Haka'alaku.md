# Overview
A vibrant but currently oppressed city in [[Shadesmar]] located where [[Cultivation's Perpendicularity]] (the Horneater Perpendicularity) manifests in the Cognitive Realm. It is built upon seven floating islands of obsidian, interconnected by a complex web of wooden platforms and shanties.

# History
Historically, Haka'alaku was a bastion of inclusivity and democracy, ruled by a senate. This era of peace ended when the [[Bloodmarks]], acting for [[The Set]], staged a violent coup to control the flow of goods and Investiture through the perpendicularity.

# Notable Features
- **The Seven Isles:** Clusters of glass land surrounded by bead seas.
	- Food Isle - A bunch of food supplies/meals taken there and basic necessities
	- Garrison Isle - Barracks and weapons/warehouses, military
	- Nexus (Docks) Isle - Entrypoint for all ships, bridges to the rest. Many piers and real-wood shipbuilding with obsidian-lined hulls.
	- Crafts slaves and Shipbuilding Isle - awef
	- Isle of a Thousand Eyes - Factional dealings, quiet worship, business and strategy meetings, creation of propaganda for [[The Set]] and the [[Bloodmarks]].
	- Mother's Isle - [[Mother's Gate]] and [[Johb]]'s domain
	- ??? - Empty
- **Dock Island:** The entry point for all ships and trade.
- **Craft/Slave Island:** Where [[Peakspren]] are forced to work on shipbuilding.
- **Garrison Island:** Contains warehouses and barracks for Set forces.
- **Island of a Thousand Eyes:** A hub for factional dealings and trade.
- **The Gilded Platforms:** A network of expensive, soulcast glasswood walkways used by the occupation forces.
- **The Slave Docks:** The deepest part of the harbor where [[Peakspren]] are chained to the oars of Scadrian-style galleys.
- **The Iron Forest**: The island with [[Mother's Gate]] has a forest of decorative iron trees, leaning in criss-cross like a jungle gym. See [[Assault on Mother's Gate]] for details.

# Secrets
The old Senate chambers beneath the central island still contain a working spanreed connected to [[Rekka]], used by the resistance to coordinate escape attempts. Mother Island is currently sealed due to a recent disturbance.
