# Overview
An upscale, unassuming studio in the [[Stoneweave]] artisan district of [[Revolar]] that specializes in the refinement of rare pigments, high-grade inks, and advanced fabric preservatives. It serves as the primary regional front for the [[Ghostbloods]] and the personal headquarters of [[Koloko]].

# Atmosphere
The shop is meticulously clean and smells faintly of sharp chemical solvents and cedarwood. Unlike the noisy industrial foundries of the [[Scarstone Quarter]], the Palette is quiet and professional. Displays of vibrant silks and bottles of jewel-toned inks line the walls, creating a facade of legitimate, high-end commerce that blends perfectly with the surrounding master-craftsman boutiques.

# Notable Features
- **The Pigment Gallery**: The front room showcases a stunning array of inks and dyes, used to vet the tastes and social standing of potential clients.
- **The Drying Racks**: A specialized chamber where fabrics are treated; the heavy ventilation system is designed to mask the more acrid scents of [[Cremwidow Poison]] refinement.
- **The Private Showroom**: A plush, soundproofed room at the back of the shop used for "consultations" with wealthy patrons. It is the primary meeting spot for the [[Undertaker]] and his senior operatives.

# Inhabitants
- [[Koloko]]: The proprietor and master chemist.
- [[Sila]]: Often present as a "clerk" or heavy-lifter, though he spends most of his time in the workshop.

# Secrets
- **The False Bottom Vats**: The heavy stone dye-vats in the basement are built over a drainage system that doubles as a secure drop-box for messages and a hidden exit into the [[Revolar]] sewers.
- **Hidden Laboratory**: Behind the racks of drying silk is a reinforced lead-lined cabinet where [[Koloko]] keeps his most volatile Nurian chemical reagents.

# Connections
- [[Undertaker]]: Has designated this as the new secure meeting point for the squad before his departure from the city.
- [[Ghostbloods]]: The organization that funded the establishment of this front.
