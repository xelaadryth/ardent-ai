# Description
A settlement mentioned as a former location for various characters and operations, now home to [[The Axelsmith]].

# Background
This town was a former base for [[Chip]]'s contact [[Sweeper]], and the location where [[Morrow]] was captured by the [[Truthkeepers]]. It is now where [[The Axelsmith]] has settled to practice his craft. 

It is also notable as the site where the mercenary [[Dahmian]] tracked and captured a [[Possessed]] individual, an act of service that resulted in his promotion to the rank of Enforcer within the [[Truthkeepers]].
