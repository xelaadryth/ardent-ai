# Overview
A strategic fortress capital city situated on the western border of the [[Sadeas Princedom]]. It is characterized by its mining industry, heavily fortified walls, and its role as the staging ground for the [[The Experimental Lightship]] demonstration.

# Background
Tomat's proximity to the volatile Veden and Reshi borders has transformed it into a critical military staging ground. It lies under [[Amaram]]'s regency. The city is currently hosting a major artifabrial exposition centered on the river-access harbor.

# Notable Features
- **The Recruitment Green:** A central square where the "Trial of the Spear" occurs.
- **The Harbor Drydock:** Location of the [[The Experimental Lightship]] test, where the Nurian-core vessel is prepared for its run to [[Bodenar]].
- **The Whispering Stone:** An ancient obsidian pillar rumored to vibrate during storms.

# Inhabitants
- [[Amaram]]: Overseeing military logistics.
- [[Tien]]: Local hero known as the "Stormblessed."
- [[Dillind]]: Young recruit under Tien's protection.
- [[Vorn]]: Lead artifabrian for the lightship project.

# Secrets
- **The Shadow Path:** A cavern system under the Whispering Stone used for smuggling.
- **Unstable Recruitment:** Corruption within the Sadeas army where recruits are sold into slavery.
