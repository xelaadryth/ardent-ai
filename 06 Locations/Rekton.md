# Overview
A small trade town located half a day's travel to the east of [[Haka'alaku]]. Rekton is largely composed of refugees—primarily [[Lightspren]] (Reachers) and escaped [[Peakspren]]—who fled the occupation of the main city.

# Atmosphere
The town is a haphazard collection of salvaged wood and canvas tents. The air is thick with a sense of watchful paranoia, as [[The Set]] frequently sends "toll collectors" to harass the residents.

# Notable Features
- **The Refugee Tents:** A sprawling camp on the edge of the town.
- **The Eastern Lookout:** A tall, spindly tower made of driftwood where [[Tessitura]] and her team watch for patrols.
- **The Silent Forge:** A communal workspace where [[Peakspren]] like [[Uum'atu]] attempt to repair tools.

# Inhabitants
- [[Uum'atu]]: A former senator of [[Haka'alaku]] and current community leader.
- [[Tessitura]]: A rebellious Lightspren navigator.

# Secrets
Rekton sits atop a natural "bubble" in the bead-sea, allowing for a small cellar where physical objects from the Physical Realm can be stored.

# Connections
- [[Haka'alaku]]: The city they hope to reclaim.
- [[Bloodmarks]]: The pirates who extort them.
