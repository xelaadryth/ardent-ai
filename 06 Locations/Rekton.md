# Overview
A small trade town located half a day's travel to the east of [[Haka'alaku]]. Rekton is largely composed of refugees—primarily [[Lightspren]] (Reachers) and escaped [[Peakspren]]—who fled the occupation of the main city. 

# Atmosphere
The town is a haphazard collection of salvaged wood and canvas tents. The air is thick with a sense of watchful paranoia, as [[The Set]] frequently sends "toll collectors" to harass the residents, though they have not yet established a permanent garrison.

# Notable Features
- **The Refugee Tents:** A sprawling camp on the edge of the town housing those who fled the galleys.
- **The Eastern Lookout:** A tall, spindly tower made of driftwood where [[Sunlight]] and her team watch for Scadrian patrols.
- **The Silent Forge:** A communal workspace where [[Peakspren]] like [[Steadfast]] attempt to repair the tools needed for a potential counter-offensive.

# Inhabitants
- [[Steadfast]]: A former senator of [[Haka'alaku]] and current community leader.
- [[Sunlight]]: A rebellious Lightspren navigator.

# Secrets
Rekton sits atop a natural "bubble" in the bead-sea, allowing for a small cellar where physical objects from the Physical Realm can be stored without immediately manifesting as beads.

# Connections
- [[Haka'alaku]]: The city they hope to reclaim.
- [[Bloodmarks]]: The pirates who extort them.
