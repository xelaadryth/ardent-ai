# Overview
A trade town in [[Shadesmar]] populated by refugees from [[Haka'alaku]]. Known locally by the [[Peakspren]] as **Rekka**.

# Notable Features
- **The Eastern Lookout:** A driftwood tower managed by [[Tessitura]].
- **The Silent Forge:** A workspace for [[Peakspren]] like [[Uum'atu]].

# Inhabitants
- [[Uum'atu]]: Community leader and former Senator.
- [[Tessitura]]: Reacher navigator and rebel contact.
