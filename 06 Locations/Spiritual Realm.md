# Overview
The Spiritual Realm, a fundamental aspect of the Cosmere, representing the immutable truths and connections between all things. It is the realm of Investiture itself and contains the blueprints of souls and the bonds that link them.

# Description
Currently, [[Kuma]] is trapped within this realm after a catastrophic transition into [[Shadesmar]], able to communicate only through a disembodied voice.
