# Description
A Regrowth spren who manifests as a series of rhythmic, ticking sounds and lights. She is highly intelligent, clinical, and protective of her bonded.

# Personality
Timbre is precise, observant, and deeply concerned with the physical world. She has a unique way of communicating, often using musical or rhythmic pulses to express complex thoughts that words struggle to capture.

# Background
Timbre originally sought to bind with [[Dahmian]], hoping that by partnering with a human, she could help offer them a chance to restore their fractured connection to the world and protect them. However, she eventually realized that this choice was foolish, as she found she could not adequately compensate for the deep-seated fears and personality clashes she encountered in him. She subsequently moved on, eventually finding a more compatible bond elsewhere.

# Connections
- [[Dahmian]]: Her former intended bond.
