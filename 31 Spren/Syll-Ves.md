# Description
Syll-Ves is an archaic, deadeye Cryptic who follows [[Iiko]] in the Cognitive Realm. Her form consists of a shifting, broken spiral face that periodically ticks like a broken clock.

# Personality
In her deadeye state, she is largely insensate, though she occasionally emits mournful, randomized screams. She follows [[Iiko]] with mechanical obedience when led by the hand. 

# Background
Before her renunciation, Syll-Ves was bonded to a Radiant who believed that sacrificing their bond would prevent the destruction of Roshar. This "greater good" renunciation broke her mind, leaving her in her current hollowed state. Recently, she has shown faint flickers of recognition, particularly when the party speaks of upholding their new oaths, suggesting her core may still be reachable.

# Goals
- Unknown; potentially searching for a connection she can no longer comprehend.

# Connections
- [[Iiko]]: Her current guide.
