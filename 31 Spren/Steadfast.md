# Description
A stony and resilient [[Peakspren]] who once served on the [[Haka'alaku]] Senate. He now leads the refugee community in [[Rekton]].

# Personality
True to his name, Steadfast is a pillar of calm in the chaotic refugee camp. He is patient, methodical, and possesses a memory for every family that was separated during the coup. He is deeply unhappy with the blockade of [[Cultivation's Perpendicularity]].

# Background
Steadfast was one of the few senators to escape during the initial raid by [[The Set]]. He carries a shard of the original Senate table as a reminder of the law he swore to uphold. He coordinates secret relief efforts to smuggle food and stormlight to the slaves in [[Haka'alaku]].

# Goals
- Liberate the enslaved Peakspren in [[Haka'alaku]].
- Restore the Senate and the inclusivity of the perpendicularity.
