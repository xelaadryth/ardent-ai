# Description
The Slipmarket is a bustling, vital district located to the southeast of [[Revolar]], near the south gate. It serves as the city's primary hub for foreign trade and maritime commerce, characterized by its busy docks and transient population [^1].

# Background
The district is defined by its unique infrastructure: stalls that fold down and stow away, allowing the market to "pop up" in a massive fashion similar to the fields of tall grass north of the city. It is a melting pot of cultures where social status is often secondary to the weight of one's purse, making it a prime location for both legitimate commerce and illicit dealings. Shipments to the market primarily arrive from the mountains to the southwest [^1].

# Hooks
- **The Abandoned Docks**: The western section of the Slipmarket contains abandoned warehouses that have served as interrogation sites for the [[Truthkeepers]] and rescue points for the [[Knights of Dusk]] [^2].
- **Merchant Activity**: The market is a known hub for rare and exotic goods, including off-world trinkets dealt by merchants like [[Zabriel]] [^3].

# Connections
- [[Revolar]]: The city in which the Slipmarket is located.
- [[Zabriel]]: A merchant who operated a stall here before his abduction by the [[Bloodmarks]] [^4].
- [[Lila]]: A laundry worker in the district who acts as an informant for the [[Knights of Dusk]] [^5].

While the Truthkeepers officially claim these individuals are controlled by malevolent external entities and represent an existential threat to the stability of Roshar, they are, in reality, often fledgling members of the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]] [^1].

The organization, particularly through its specialized shadow faction known as the [[Silent Oath]], hunts these individuals to prevent what they fear could be a repeat of the ancient Desolations [^2].
