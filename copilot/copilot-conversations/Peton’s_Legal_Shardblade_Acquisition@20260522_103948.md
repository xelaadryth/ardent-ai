# Description
A high-ranking official within the Truthkeepers who balances a rigid sense of duty with the messy reality of leading "outsider" units. While he presents himself as a strict traditionalist, he harbors a secret ambition to reform the organization's brutal methods from within.

# Appearance
Tall and sharp-faced with bright blue eyes and a neatly cropped hair and beard. He is typically seen equipped for duty, wearing a sidesword and carrying a shield. He keeps a Shardblade concealed, drawing it only when absolutely necessary to survive or to exert authority in dire circumstances.

# Personality
A rigid traditionalist and a stickler for order, structure, and established norms. He possesses a surprising hidden depth: he will wholeheartedly respect a democratic vote, provided it strictly adheres to the established rules. He is deeply pragmatic, believing that the ends—improving the Truthkeepers' dark methods—justify the means of his own deception.

# Background
Originally an Enforcer, Peton’s dedication to the law saw him promoted to the rank of Adjudicator. He has a habit of intentionally assigning himself to the most difficult or "unconventional" units to ensure they remain aligned with the law. 

During a mission to capture a "Possessed" target, Peton and his trusted Enforcer, [[Ojog]], encountered an individual wielding a Shardblade. The target claimed the blade was "screaming," a sign of the corruption the Truthkeepers are sworn to purge. In a desperate, close-quarters fight, Peton saved Ojog’s life and successfully neutralized the target. Rather than reporting the weapon to the Silent Oath, Peton claimed the blade for himself. He keeps its existence hidden, knowing that the Silent Oath would seize it and likely execute him for his unauthorized possession. He views the blade as a necessary tool to eventually challenge the organization's internal corruption.

# Goals
- **Maintaining Order:** Ensure society functions by established rules and traditional structures.
- **Leadership:** Guide his "flock" toward the Truthkeeper cause through discipline and example.
- **Internal Reform:** Secretly accumulate enough influence and power to steer the Truthkeepers away from their most extreme, lethal practices.

# Connections
- [[Ojog]]: His most trusted Enforcer and the only person who knows the truth about the Shardblade.
