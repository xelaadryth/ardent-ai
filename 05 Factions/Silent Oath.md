# Description
A shadow organization operating within the higher echelons of the [[Truthkeepers]], specialized in the clinical elimination of high-priority "Possessed" targets.

In reality, the Silent Oath is composed of high-ranking members of the Skybreaker order. They leverage the legal and religious infrastructure of the Truthkeepers to identify fledgling Knights Radiant. They require legal grounds—often in the form of witnesses to supernatural crimes—before executing judgment.

# Goals
- **Maintain "Order":** Prevent the return of the Desolation by executing those who bond spren.
- **Legal Precedent:** Secure witnesses to Radiant surges to justify their purge under Alethi law.
- **Strategic Purge:** Target and eliminate dangerous "Possessed" based on intelligence reports and legal precedents.

# Allies & Rivals
- [[Truthkeepers]]: The front of their organization.
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: Ideologically opposed.
