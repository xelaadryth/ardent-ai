In session 26 (The Bead Sea), the party starts talking with Varo Three-Knives, where he smugly requests the payment of 500 marks for saving the civilians and other Knights of Dusk escapees; if the Knights of Dusk lost, then the Bloodmarks would have crushed all hopes of the survivors. If the Knights of Dusk won, then the Bloodmarks could make money escorting the survivors away; they already got paid as mercenaries by the Vamah Princedom, so that way whoever wins, the Bloodmarks profit. However the party, especially Galeth, refused to pay up. Varo said it wasn't his decision, and indicated that his boss, Rissa Vale, was on the ship. During this argument, High Enforcer Aylen approached the ship and asked if there were any Radiants on board the ship. While trying to stall, the party backed up into the secret storage compartment of the ship with the Nurian gems, where they were surprised to find Hoid. He advised that the party escape into Shadesmar with the power of the Nurian gems. As Aylen was reading off the list of crimes the party committed, justifying their arrest and likely execution though really just an excuse to execute fledgling Knights Radiant, Kuma and Mallow grabbed the Nurian gems and the party (including Renna and Gorlo) grabbed on to each other, and Rissa Vale and Varo grabbed on too. The stormlight was not enough, and it seemed to be drawing more than just stormlight, draining Kuma and Mallow's lifeforce directly, before Kuma knocked Mallow's hand away. In a blaze of light, the party and the entire boat appeared in Shadesmar (although not the other people on the boat), and the party met their bonded spren in their original forms for the first time. However, Kuma wasn't there; Viscose just said that he was "gone". However the pouch of Rissa's tracking disks was left on the ground. There was enough food for about 3 days on the ship. Rissa was threatened but she said she was the only person that knew directions in Shadesmar. Also the party discovered Kaizen, a kid, was also on the ship, and he claimed to be the Hero of Ages (and the captain of the ship), that he's also from Scadrial (wearing a metal mask in the style of a kestrel), and that he's a Suit of the Set (which means he outranks Rissa who is only a Cycle of the Set). He was chitchatting with Iiko and trying to show off, and used Allomancy based on moving and manipulating metals. The party made oars from scraps of the ship's railing, popped holes in the sides of the ship as oarlocks using Division, and used Abrasion to slide the ship into the bead sea. After 2 days of travel, they noticed some big purple thing in the distance, and then saw another ship in the distance, and Rissa smiles.

In session 27 (Battle of Ships and Spren), the party engages the other ship called the Wandering Eye. Fina and Galeth are rowing below-decks with the spren and others. Oar-powered and larger than Varo's ship, Kaizen and Rissa immediately use steelpushing to try to jump to the other ship, but Mallow grabs on, wrenching Rissa's arm, and the three of them end up crossing up onto the larger ship. They notice the other ship is captained by Arbiter Jalis, and N'tal immediately chases him down and sparks fly as Mallow threatens Rissa with a knife. Iiko tries to talk everyone down, but goaded by Jalis, N'tal attacks, and Mallow stabs Rissa, shocking herself as she's never drawn blood at such close range before, usually only firing arrows from afar. N'tal disintegrates one of Jalis's arms, and then the other, before painspren and angerspren attack the ship. The painspren target Jalis, killing him even though N'tal was trying to keep him alive for future questioning. The angerspren target Rissa, Kaizen, and Mallow, completely bypassing Iiko until they attack back by bonking one with a stick. Varo also joins in the fight once the spren arrive, and the battle ends with no serious injuries other than Rissa falling unconscious from her wounds. The party then checks below decks to discover a dozen peakspren chained to the oars.

In session 28, the party free the peakspren, and immediately earn the ire of Vun'akal by Galeth mentioning that he's a Stoneward, and that the party are Radiants. Vun'akal said that the Knights Radiant broke the peakspren's trust in the past leaving hundreds of deadeyes. He asks Kun'ahu why he helps the Oathbreakers; perhaps as penance for a sin committed? Kun'ahu replies that between humans and spren, someone has to be the first to try to trust the other, and that the peakspren cannot stay forever stagnant as stone; Kun'ahu feels a shadow lies over Roshar, and it is time for change. Galeth grudgingly wins Vun'akal's trust, and he fills the party in on the situation at Haka'alaku, which has been captured by the Set via tricks, taking hostages, and tearing down the original city there weeks or months ago. Haka'alaku of the seven isles was built around Mother's Gate, which the party learns can bring them back to the physical realm. He knows the leader of the nearby town of Rekka (the corrected name of Rekton) named Uum'atu, another peakspren. The party decides to leave the sinking Deepen Light beached while sailing the Wandering Eye to Rekka, rather than resting due to the dwindling of their stormlight. On their ship, N'tal finds weapons of the same strange obsidian-like material the land of Shadesmar is made of, and arms the peakspren. While there, they see a few peakspren and humans, but the down seems almost empty and despondent. The humans are willing to trade, but the party goes to see Uum'atu first. He trusts Vun'akal, and tells the party he used to be a Senator of Haka'alaku. He says to seek out Tessitura, a reacher on the outskirts of town, who has contacts with other rebels. The party find her lean-to by an obsidian-tree forest, and Cadence helps the party quickly earn her trust. They decide that the reachers will bring any allies they can find to fight the Set ships guarding Mother's Gate, including the rebels in Rekka itself. The party questions Rissa more and finds out details on the mysterious Commander guarding the portal named Johb, and learn that he is an iron puller. They also learn a bit about Allomancy and Feruchemy, and that the Set is in the area to control trade through Cultivation's Perpendicularity. They learn from Kaizen that he ran away from home and from the Ghostbloods to join the Set because they treated him better.
