Create NPC files for the following potential allies/enemies:

"Johb", presumed to be a Malwish iron misting (Lurcher) who wields a rapier and dagger. He fights like a rogue, surprising enemies by throwing his dagger or even his rapier to distract them, before pulling his knives back through them from behind. He is a member of the Bloodmark Corsairs, a lieutenant who wants to make Stormlight and artifabrials available to everyone. By securing funding and a Bloodmark foothold on Roshar, he acquires numerous fabrials and can trigger them remotely. Deeply interested in taking Kuma's Prized Fabrial as he knows he can use it better. Joking and taunting personality, but not badhearted. More of a philosopher at heart. His secret is that he was not born an iron misting; he was the result of the Ghostbloods experimenting on him with Hemalurgy; they purposely killed an iron misting with a steel needle on the end of a knife through the eye and brain, and then inserted the needle between Johb's shoulder blades as a teenager. It was a massive success, and Johb was elated at his newfound powers, until he found out that his mother had killed someone to give him the powers. After that he cut ties with the Ghostbloods and joined the Bloodmarks instead, and over time the spike has slowly corrupted him with the intent of Ruin. The Bloodmarks seek to protect Scadrial by destroying and taking from other places, where the Ghostbloods try to preserve and nurture others to achieve their goals. They try not to directly oppose each other but often are "discordant" with each other.

"Kaizen", a rare Malwish Mistborn who was rewarded with Lerasium as a child because his father sacrificed himself to save Thaidakar and the Ghostbloods from certain destruction. However in typical rebellious teenage fashion, he decided to run away from home and took up with the Bloodmarks, convinced that he's the chosen one. He grew up so spoiled by well-meaning friends of his father that he never learned right from wrong, and is just selfish and wants to be the hero in the prophecies, like the Hero of Ages, as he thinks he was destined to be. He never seriously practiced any of his abilities as he's not used to hard work, and trusts that he will awaken them when the time is right. The Bloodmarks convinced him to travel to Scadrial because he can win a Shardblade as every hero needs a magic sword. He always wears a mask and is still confused why other primitives don't wear them.

"Marcus", a Veden bounty hunter on Roshar. He is semi-retired and has seen too much death in his time, but he continues to kill as he feels like it's the only thing he's good for. He has lost sight of his values and given up on himself, and tells himself in his pessimistic nihilism that at least death through a Shardblade is a clean and painless death, far better than experiencing the harshness of the world. He used to do it for the thrill of the hunt and the challenge of fighting famous warriors, but as he has aged he realizes he's just saving them from living the lonely life of a killer like himself. He is not affiliated with any specific organization but the Bloodmarks will put a bounty on the party after they escape after they attempt to save jory at the abandoned docks.

"Nathaniel", an extremely powerful and skilled Alethi fighter who wields a halberd. He thinks himself very noble and brave, wanting to help people, but the truth is that he's a coward at heart that sneaks away as soon as it gets dangerous, telling himself that by living to see another day, he'll preserve his strength to protect others another time, playing the long game. He is drawn to the party by a Connection with the Identity of "The Axe", which is actually a halberd that Galeth-son-Thald carries. His bloodline can bring out its true potential as it was forged with the spirit of his ancestors infused into the tree the wood was cut from, making it lighter, quicker, and more deadly in his hands. As soon as a fight looks dangerous or something unexpected occurs, he sneaks away as carefully as he can. Avoids dangerous enemies and fights the weak and untrained. Also has low self esteem and thinks himself completely incapable, which partially explains why he leaves when he "knows he's outmatched."
