Create an NPC page for Gorlo, who is an NPC who recently joined the party. He was originally Thaylen, and as a lad joined a pirate crew for fun thinking it would be fun. The friend who recruited him was killed in a bad raid where much of the crew was decimated, and the new captain of the ship was a lot more brutal. Realizing he had too much blood on his hands to leave, complicit in too many crimes, Gorlo realized his destiny was crime. He managed to escape that crew and joined the Bloodmarks, which wasn't quite as bad, and tried not to do anything too evil, mostly intimidating and roughing up folks, or escort and delivery jobs rather than too much violence. Piggy and Fang were the other two in his little friend group in the Bloodmarks. Gorlo thought of Piggy as a little brother to be helped, protected, and taught, since Piggy was afraid and wouldn't have survived in the gang without him. Fang tried to be a good person but when his anger was up he would do heinous things that he would regret later. After Piggy and Fang were killed helping the party escape from Tash the Hand, Gorlo put his life in the party's hands for judgment again, and is oddly attached to Fina, who he credits with his life for some reason as she remembered his name.

Make sure you have detailed descriptions of his appearance and clothing as a former pirate as well as some identifying characteristics, now trying to turn over a new leaf. Gorlo is intelligent and streetsmart (though not learned like a scholar), strong and quick (though he doesn't like to fight with his knives), but traitor to the Bloodmarks (though he is loyal to the party for sparing him where everyone else has only failed him). He is a mirror image to the party; he turned his back on the organization he didn't want to be in, trying to join the other side despite harming the party's organization (Knights of Dusk) previously.
