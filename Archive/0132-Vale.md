Create an NPC document for Arbiter Rissa Vale, a lighteyed intelligence operative in the Bloodmarks whose name the party will hear but will not yet meet in person. She's originally from Scadrial and works with offworlders like Arbiter Jalis. She's the one commanding both Tash and Varo.

**Appearance**

- Dark coat
- Ledger chained to her belt
- Never raises her voice

**Personality**

- Observant
- Patient
- Knows too much

She studies the party members.

She points out their habits.

Accurately guesses their thoughts or what they care about.

### Sample Dialogue

To Galeth:

> "You're probably sizing us up for a fight'."

To Iiko:

> "Try to think of how to use your Possessed powers to get out of this one again, aren't you?"

To Fina:

> "You'll need to work on that tell; breaking eye contact before you lie is a little too obvious."

### Why She Works

Creates paranoia.

Can escape and become a recurring antagonist.
