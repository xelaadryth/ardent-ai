Create a "03 Shadesmar Arc" that I plan to use in place of the Stormblessed Arc. The out-of-game reason is that traveling to Tomat over land is pretty boring as it covers area the party has been before. The in-game inciting incident is when the party tries to escape the Silent Oath during The Darkstone Siege, after the party escapes to a boat, they are almost caught, and Kuma speaks the words and somehow uses Transportation to bring the entire party into Shadesmar (with the help of Mallow + maybe some kind of amplifier like a stash of Nurian gems that Zabriel stole on board the boat). Doing this consumes all the stormlight the entire party is carrying. While in Shadesmar, the party must overcome several obstacles such as bead sea predators, dangerous spren, a spren city (where the party can't prove radiants are returning because they're out of stormlight), and most importantly Bloodmarks pirates. Unlike previously encountered Bloodmarks (local Roshar branch), worldhopper Bloodmarks are originally from Scadrial, and the pirate captains and some special crewmates are allomancers or feruchemists. Temeret Malice is their leader and is a twinborn with an interesting combination. The party must brave these perils while trading for food/water with the help of some Ghostblood agents to make it to the Horneater Peaks perpendicularity, where they will encounter Malice herself.
