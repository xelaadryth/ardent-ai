Write a session document for session 25. The party returned to the Knights of Dusk base, where they were informed by General Spade that insurgents from among the darkeyed recruits had stolen much of the stormlight stash of gems and escaped. The party secured the rest of the base, checking on the escape tunnel and discovered that there was fighting and looting in the residential area, and that the Bloodmarks had actually captured the front gatehouse for a short period of time before the party returned before General Spade and Iiko reclaimed control of it.

The base was attacked by the Bloodmarks led by Tash the Hound, the Vamah Guard led by General Odrin Lesh and Brightlord Juran, and later surprise attacked by the Truthkeepers led by Ojog and Adjudicator Milara. The party fought enemies off on the top of the fortifications with ballistae, cut down siege ropes as the Bloodmarks climbed up. Tash the Hound was punctured by a ballista bolt, stone spears, and eventually pinned to the ground, impaled, by Galeth-son-Thald.

Iiko used illusions of ballistae to delay and prevent the Truthkeeper forces from breaking in even after cutting holes in the sides of the base with Ojog's shardblade, eventually forcing most of the Truthkeeper forces to surrender after conjuring illusions of reinforcements and more ballistae surrounding them.

The Vamah Guard had a chull-pulled battering ram which Mallow dug underneath and sunk the front wheels into the ground. They were later joined by Ojog who helped General Odrin Lesh and Brightlord Juran's elite guard to cut a hole through the tunnel dug by Mallow, but were met by an inferno of flames on vines and oil, decimating their forces.

In the end the Ojog surrendered, although still declaring his loyalty to the Truthkeepers saying he would not betray his word. Brightlord Juran surrendered from cowardice. General Odrin Lesh challenged General Spade to one last battle, one-on-one, and was crumbled to ash by the surge of Division.

They questioned Tash the Hound and the Truthkeepers, but the Silent Oath arrived, at least 20 of their number flying in sporting Shardblades. General Spade told the party to evacuate the base and escape out the back tunnel. The party escaped to see boats brought in, led by Nathaniel, evacuating the refugees. The party got in the last boat, and when about to board was stopped by Istrum. She pretended not to know they were surgebinders, and was just here to retrieve Ojog, who she said would make a great recruit for the Silent Oath, who she said were the Order of Knights known as the Skybreakers, the only Radiants to not betray humanity. As they sailed away, they saw High Enforcer Aylen fly in and confer with Ojog after he used Division to reduce the entire Knights of Dusk base to ash, and the party realized Aylen was actually the Herald Nale. The session ended with the party realizing that Varo Three-Knives was on the ship (disguised Deepen Light), and Ojog saying a few words to High Enforcer Aylen, who then turned his dangerous gaze on the ship, presumably to chase down the party.

End of Arc 2: Knights of Dusk.
