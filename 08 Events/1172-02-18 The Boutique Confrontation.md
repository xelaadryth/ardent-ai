# Description
A high-stakes social confrontation in a [[Skyglass District]] boutique where [[Renna Palir]] was confronted by her estranged fiancé, [[Brightlord Juran]]. The event revealed Renna's past as a runaway bride and the trauma of losing her younger sister.

# The Confrontation
While the party was being fitted for noble attire to blend in for an upcoming mission, [[Brightlord Juran]] entered the shop. He recognized [[Renna Palir]] immediately, labeling her a "runaway bride" and demanding she fulfill her family's debt-ridden betrothal contract. 

# Key Moments
- **Protective Instincts:** [[Renna Palir]] instinctively shielded [[Iiko]], treating them as a surrogate for her deceased younger sister.
- **Shaming the Grief:** Juran mocked Renna’s grief, claiming her service to the [[Truthkeepers]] was a cowardly attempt to hide from her failures and her inability to save her sister.
- **Fina's Flashpoint:** [[Fina]], recognizing the parallels to her own escape from a political marriage alliance, stood her ground against Juran. She blocked his path and forced him to release Renna, drawing on her own history of escaping such a cage.

# Outcomes
- **Shared Bond:** [[Fina]] and [[Renna Palir]] recognized each other as kindred spirits, significantly strengthening their trust.
- **Noble Enmity:** [[Brightlord Juran]] remains a threat in [[Revolar]], vowing to alert Renna's father and force her to fulfill the contract.
- **Iiko's Observation:** [[Iiko]] witnessed the depth of Renna's trauma and her instinctual need to protect those she considers family.
