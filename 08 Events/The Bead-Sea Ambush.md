# Description
A harrowing survival encounter in the bead-sea of [[Shadesmar]] following the party's arrival. A massive, predatory spren resembling a translucent sea-serpent stalks the party's vessel, drawn by the residual investiture of the [[Nurian Gems]] used during the shift.

# Details
- **The Buoyancy Crisis:** The party's physical boat is not inherently buoyant on the shifting glass beads of the Cognitive Realm. They must coordinate to prevent the hull from being swamped while the "Glass-Tooth Serpent" circles below.
- **The Predator:** A Tier 3 Spren Threat. It strikes with a crystalline maw that can "shatter" the cognitive identity of physical objects, causing parts of the boat to revert into individual beads upon impact.
- **The Stormlight Gamble:** [[Zabriel]] is forced to distribute his remaining stash of [[Nurian Gems]] to the party. The players must decide whether to use the light for immediate defense or conserve it for the long trek to a spren city.

# Participants
- [[Kuma]]: Responsible for reinforcing the ship's structure using the surge of Transformation.
- [[Mallow]]: Utilizing her Willshaper resonance to navigate the ship through the bead-drifts.
- [[Zabriel]]: Providing the power source and acting as a lookout.
- [[N'tal]]: Leading the martial defense against the serpent's breaches.

# Outcomes
- **Resource Depletion:** A successful defense will likely drain several high-capacity [[Nurian Gems]], increasing the party's vulnerability.
- **Cognitive Scars:** The vessel may sustain permanent damage that requires specialized spren-crafting to repair once they reach safety.
