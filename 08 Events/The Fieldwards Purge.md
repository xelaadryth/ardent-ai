# Description
A planned joint operation between the [[Truthkeepers]] and the city guard to dismantle a [[Knights of Dusk]] cell operating out of the grain silos in the [[Revolar]] [[Fieldwards]]. The event is a calculated orchestration by [[Ardent Surusa]] to infiltrate the rebellion's higher leadership.

# Details
- **The Assignment:** [[Adjudicator Peton]] tasks the squad, [[Senny]], and [[Neval]] with conducting a surgical strike on a suspected rebel gathering. The objective is to capture the cell leader and recover logistical maps.
- **The Setting:** The silos of the western [[Fieldwards]]. The environment is filled with flammable grain dust and narrow, vertical scaffolding, complicating both Surgebinding and conventional combat.
- **The Warning:** [[Ardent Surusa]] arrives at the silo meet-up just as the raid begins. She warns the gathered rebels that she was "late to the gathering" and narrowly avoided a [[Truthkeepers]] patrol. She claims she tried to find help but "didn't know how to contact other cells" in time.
- **The Deception:** Surusa's true goal is to be "rescued" by the fleeing rebels and taken to their primary hideout, potentially the [[Darkstone Forest]]. She uses the chaos of the raid to demonstrate her "loyalty" while secretly identifying the survivors for future neutralization.

# Participants
- [[Truthkeepers]]: The party, [[Senny]], [[Neval]], and city guard detachments.
- [[Knights of Dusk]]: A local cell and their informant [[Lila]].
- [[Ardent Surusa]]: Acting as the "heroic" informant for the rebellion.

# Outcomes
- **Infiltration:** If the party allows the rebels to escape with Surusa, she successfully gains access to the [[Knights of Dusk]] internal communication network.
- **Sabotage:** Surusa begins to quietly disappear or alter maps found at the site, misdirecting the rebellion's future supply raids.
- **Suspicion:** The party may notice that Surusa’s "lucky escape" from the [[Truthkeepers]] perimeter was suspiciously smooth, or find her in possession of camp-issued signaling spheres.
