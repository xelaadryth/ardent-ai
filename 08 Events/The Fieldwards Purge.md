# Description
A joint operation between the [[Truthkeepers]] and the city guard to dismantle a [[Knights of Dusk]] cell operating out of the grain silos in the [[Revolar]] [[Fieldwards]].

# Outcomes
- **Capture:** Rebels Otson and Bellus were captured by [[Senny]] and [[Neval]].
- **Rescue:** The party successfully intercepted the prisoner transport to the [[Vamah Archivum]], using an illusion of [[Adjudicator Peton]] to divert the prisoners to [[Knights of Dusk]] custody.
- **Infiltration:** [[Ardent Surusa]] utilized the chaos to deepen her cover within the rebellion.
