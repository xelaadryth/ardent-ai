# Description
A pivotal social and moral encounter in the bead-sea of [[Shadesmar]]. A patrolling honorspren vessel, the *Cloud-Seeker*, discovers the party's stranded boat. What begins as a rescue operation turns into a tense standoff when the honorspren realize they are dealing with nascent [[Orders of the Knights Radiant|Knights Radiant]].

# The Encounter
The honorspren, led by Captain Notum, initially offer assistance to the "strays from the physical realm." However, during the initial inspection of the ship's damage, the party's spren—[[Verdae]], [[Pyre]], [[Viscose]], and [[Cadence]]—manifest to protect their partners. The sight of sapient Nahel bonds in their early stages triggers a visceral, ancestral trauma in the honorspren crew.

# The Dialogue
The honorspren react with visible horror, their glowing blue forms flickering with jagged sparks of agitation.

- **Captain Notum:** "Stop! By the Stormfather, do not speak another word of your First Ideal. We still hear the silence that followed those words a thousand years ago."
- **Officer Sylas:** "You are the children of the traitors. You hold the broken souls of our kin in your hands and call it a 'bond'. It is a parasite, nothing more!"
- **Captain Notum:** "We were coming to mend your sails, but why would we sharpen the knife that is meant for our own throats? To help you is to invite the return of the slaughter. The [[Day of Recreance]] was not a mistake; it was a mercy. Why would you bring the madness back to the sky?"

# The Trial
The party must convince the honorspren that this era is different. They must prove that their spren are not captives but willing partners in the fight against the [[Bloodmarks]] and the coming [[Everstorm]].

- **Kuma (Elsecaller):** Must demonstrate through [[Viscose]] that their bond is built on logic and mutual advancement.
- **Mallow (Willshaper):** Must appeal to [[Cadence]]'s hatred of slavery to show that the Nahel bond is the ultimate expression of shared freedom, not servitude.
- **N'tal (Dustbringer):** Must show that his restraint with [[Pyre]] is what prevents the destruction they so fear.

# Outcomes
- **Success:** The honorspren grudgingly use their command over the bead-sea to reinforce the party's hull and provide a small amount of Stormlight, though they refuse to accompany the "Radiants" further.
- **Failure:** The honorspren abandon the party to the bead-drifts, forcing them to rely on [[Zabriel]]'s remaining [[Nurian Gems]] and potentially attracting the attention of [[Grand Justicar Malice]].
