# Description
The squad chooses to sever their ties with the [[Truthkeepers]] and aid the [[Knights of Dusk]] in a desperate rescue at the abandoned docks in the western [[Slipmarket]]. The objective is to retrieve [[Jory Spadis]] from the prison ship *Deepen Light* before a scheduled interrogation begins.

# Events
- **The Intel:** The party learned from [[Varo Three-Knives]] that the interrogation time has been moved up to "shortly," necessitating an immediate strike.
- **The Vessel:** The *Deepen Light* was identified as the transport vessel, confirmed to be carrying [[Nurian Gems]] alongside its human cargo.
- **The Bait:** The party learns that [[Senny]], [[Neval]], [[Cazo]], and [[Hine]] were supposed to be dispatched by [[Adjudicator Peton]] to interrogate a [[Possessed]] boy tonight.
- **The Passphrase**: The Bloodmarks on the scene use two different passphrase.
	- How many marks to pass? 8, 16, or using the word "blood" like "only Bloodmarks pass."
	- What ruins mar the graves? Only rust in crimson shades.
- **The Skirmish:** (Ongoing/Planned) The party must confront the Bloodmarks, led by [[Varo Three-Knives]] and [[Tash The Hound]].
