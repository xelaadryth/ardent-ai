# Description
The squad chooses to sever their ties with the [[Truthkeepers]] and aid the [[Knights of Dusk]] in a desperate rescue at the abandoned docks in the western [[Slipmarket]]. This event serves as a public declaration of allegiance, where the party must rescue [[Jory Spadis]] and a lucky merchant named [[Zabriel]].

# Events
- **The Rescue Mission:** Acting on information from the [[Knights of Dusk]], the party infiltrates a [[Truthkeepers]] interrogation site near some abandoned warehouses in the empty western docks. Their goal is to extract [[Jory Spadis]] before he is executed. 
- **The Sidequest:** While securing [[Jory Spadis]], the party finds the merchant [[Zabriel]] held in a secondary pen. Saving him is vital as he possesses rare knowledge and a knack for finding the obscure.
- **Truthkeeper Reinforcements:** Just as victory seems possible, [[Ojog]] and [[Renna Palir]] arrive with a large wave of Truthkeeper archers. They assume the party is carrying out sanctioned orders until the party reveals their true intent. [[Ojog]] sadly orders his agents to kill them all.
- **Knights of Dusk Reinforcements:** At the darkest moment, [[Beric Quinn]] and his skirmishers cut a path out, sacrificing himself so the party can escape.
- **Zabriel's Reward:** After rescue, [[Zabriel]] points the party toward a cache of curiosities in [[Revolar]] he stashed from his travels to [[Tomat]], including rare specimens of [[Nurian Gems]].
