# Description
The squad chooses to sever their ties with the [[Truthkeepers]] and aid the [[Knights of Dusk]] in a desperate rescue at an abandoned warehouse in the western [[Slipmarket]].

# Outcomes
- **Rescue:** [[Jory Spadis]] is successfully retrieved, though he is traumatized by the experience.
- **The First Ideal:** [[Galeth-son-Thald]] spoke the First Ideal of the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant|Stonewards]], committing himself to the path of the Radiant.
- **Defection:** The party is now officially outlaws to the [[Vamah Princedom]] and targets of the [[Truthkeepers]].
- **Rivalry:** [[Tash The Hound]] has claimed a trophy from [[Galeth-son-Thald]] and is obsessively tracking him.
- **Casualties:** [[Adjudicator Peton]] was severely wounded by the party before their escape.
