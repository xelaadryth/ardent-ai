# Description
The party is in a high-end boutique in the Skyglass District getting fitted for noble attire to blend in for an upcoming mission [[The Cinder Evacuation]]. Renna is nervously checking the windows, trying to stay out of sight, when the bell above the door rings.
In walks [[Brightlord Juran]]. He’s impeccably dressed, arrogant, and flanked by two armored House guards. He's here on his own business, but as his eyes scan the shop, they lock directly onto Renna.
### Phase 1: The Confrontation
Juran Minarim stops, a cruel, satisfied smile spreading across his face.

> **Juran:** *"Well, well, Renna Palir. My runaway bride, hiding in a tailor's shop like a common thief."*

Renna freezes, her face draining of color. She steps back, her hand reaching out to pull **Iiko** slightly behind her.
> **Renna**: "What do you want?"

Juran steps forward, looking at her with cold, calculating eyes.

> **Juran:** *"Do you have any idea how much embarrassment you've caused me? Running away from our betrothal like a petulant child? Your family’s house is crumbling, Renna. You need my family's backing, and I need your father’s signature. I didn't pay your bride-price just for you to vanish into the night for months."*

### Phase 2: Shaming Her Grief
Renna trembles, but tries to hold her ground, keeping herself between Juran and Iiko.
> **Renna:** *"I'm not going back, Juran. I never wanted this marriage. After Anner... passed, I wanted to find a way to actually **help** people. I won't be your pawn in some game."*
> 
Juran lets out a sharp, mocking laugh.

> **Juran:** *"Help people? You couldn't even save your own sister, you've abandoned your family, and now you're playing dress-up with sellswords. You're a coward, Renna. You ran because you failed at being a sister, and by running you've failed at being a daughter too."*
> 
### Phase 3: Fina’s Flashpoint
Juran reaches out to grab Renna’s arm to drag her out of the shop.
This hits every single one of **Fina’s** triggers. He is an arrogant noble enforcing an arranged marriage, treating a woman like a transaction, and mocking someone for choosing freedom over a political cage.
When Fina or Iiko tries to step in—blocking Juran's path and forcing his hand away from Renna—Juran sneers at them.
> **Juran:** *"Out of my way, lackeyed/girl. You have no idea who you're dealing with. This woman belongs to my estate."*

Fina now has the perfect opportunity to draw her line in the sand, drawing on her own history of escaping a political marriage to look this man in the eye.
### Phase 4: The Retreat
Seeing Fina stand firm, backed up by the rest of a heavily armed, highly dangerous adventuring party, Juran realizes a public brawl in the Skyglass District will ruin his reputation. He steps back, adjusting his cuffs, his eyes narrowing.
> **Juran:** *"Enjoy your little playground adventure, Renna. But Revolar is my city. You can't hide with these gutter-scum forever. Your father will hear of this, and by the Heralds, you **will** fulfill the contract."*

He turns and sweeps out of the boutique with his guards.

Renna turns to Iiko, "Are you okay?", and then she starts hyperventilating.
## Why This Set-Up Shines:
 * **The Fina Connection:** Fina isn't just defending a random NPC anymore; she is actively staring down the exact monster she ran away from in her own homeland. It instantly bonds Fina and Renna as kindred spirits.
- The Iiko Dynamic: By having Renna instinctively protect Iiko when Juran steps up, the players see that Renna's motherly/older-sister instinct isn't just a quirk—it's how she copes with the sister she lost.
- The Stakes: Renna is no longer a passive guide. She is a woman hiding from a powerful, vindictive local noble who now knows she is in the city. The party will naturally want to protect her from him.
