# Description
The climactic final confrontation of the [[02 Knights of Dusk Arc]]. The Vamah Guard and [[Truthkeepers]] assault the insurgency's base. The party's escape boat is nearly intercepted by the [[Silent Oath]], triggering a desperate shift into [[Shadesmar]].

# Outcomes
- **The Breach of Realities:** When cornered by [[High Enforcer Aylen]], [[Kuma]] speaks the Second Ideal of the Elsecallers. Using a stash of [[Nurian Gems]] stolen by [[Zabriel]], he and [[Mallow]] activate the surge of Transportation.
- **The Cognitive Transition:** The entire party and their vessel are pulled into [[Shadesmar]], consuming all available Stormlight and leaving them stranded in the bead-sea.
- **The Loophole of Mercy:** [[Istrum]] chooses inaction, allowing the party to vanish into the Cognitive Realm rather than pursuing them into the unknown.
