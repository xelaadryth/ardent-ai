# Description
Witness Dahmian, seeking his official promotion to Enforcer, leads the squad to an alleyway in the [[Slipmarket]]. He presents an individual he has identified as [[Possessed]] and orders the squad to carry out the "enforcement" as his final vetting process.

# Personal Revelations
As the party observes Dahmian in the field, suppressed memories from their time in Nuria resurface, painting a picture of a man who has always been a restless wanderer:
- **[[Mallow]]:** Remembers Dahmian as the highly efficient Witness who successfully captured a [[Possessed]] individual in [[Salimset]] shortly after their arrival on Roshar.
- **[[Galeth-son-Thald]]:** Recalls seeing Dahmian speaking privately with [[Sila]] and [[Luggan]] at the [[Truthkeeper Camp]]. Given his mercenary background, Galeth suspects Dahmian was the mysterious benefactor who paid [[Luggan]] to assassinate [[Doug (Guddy)]].
- **[[Fina]]:** Remembers him as a charismatic combat stunt actor in a theatrical troupe that frequently performed for the Nurian royal castle.
- **[[N'tal]]:** Recognizes him as a reliable, if distant, hired guard for various merchant caravans transporting goods across the Nurian plains.
- **[[Iiko]]:** Remembers his face from their temple in Nuria, where he frequently handled heavy supply deliveries.

# Outcomes
- **Promotion:** Upon the completion of the task, Dahmian is officially raised to the rank of Enforcer by [[Adjudicator Peton]].
- **Deepening Schism:** The realization of Dahmian's potential involvement in the camp murders, combined with his ruthlessness during the trial, pushes the party closer to the [[Knights of Dusk]].
