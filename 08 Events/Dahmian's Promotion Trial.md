# Description
Witness [[Dahmian]], seeking his official promotion to Enforcer, leads the squad to an alleyway in the [[Slipmarket]]. He presents Elara, an individual he has identified as [[Possessed]] and awaits the squad to show him what "enforcement" looks like. 

# Outcomes
- **Promotion:** Dahmian is promoted following the successful (if deceptive) completion of the trial.
- **Rebel Link:** Elara is revealed as a member of the [[Knights of Dusk]]. The party warned her of the [[Truthkeepers]]' intent, allowing her to escape.
- **Nurian Recognition:** The squad confirms Dahmian's background as a fellow Nurian and his rejection of a spren bond.
