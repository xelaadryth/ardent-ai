# Description
Witness Dahmian, seeking his official promotion to Enforcer, leads the squad to an alleyway in the [[Slipmarket]]. He presents Elara, an individual he has identified as [[Possessed]] and awaits the squad to show him what "enforcement" looks like. She has mentioned that she hears voices and that the sick and injured feel much better after she feeds them; she believes with better nutrition everyone would be much healthier, not realizing their improved health is a result of her powers. He asks the party if they can help her with the voice she hears, as part of his Enforcer promotion. If pressed, she reveals she's a member of the Knights of Dusk, and that the people will remember, just as the Knights of Dusk remembered the people.

# Personal Revelations
As the party observes Dahmian in the field, suppressed memories from their time in Nuria resurface, painting a picture of a man who has always been a restless wanderer:
- **[[Mallow]]:** Remembers Dahmian as the highly efficient Witness who successfully captured a [[Possessed]] individual in [[Salimset]] shortly after their arrival on Roshar.
- **[[Galeth-son-Thald]]:** Recalls seeing Dahmian speaking privately with [[Sila]] and [[Luggan]] at the [[Truthkeeper Camp]].
- **[[Fina]]:** Remembers him as a charismatic combat stunt actor in a theatrical troupe that frequently performed for the Nurian royal castle.
- **[[N'tal]]:** Recognizes him as a reliable, if distant, hired guard for various merchant caravans transporting goods across the Nurian plains.
- **[[Iiko]]:** Remembers his face from their temple in Nuria, where he frequently handled heavy supply deliveries.

# Outcomes
- **Promotion:** Upon the completion of the task, Dahmian is officially raised to the rank of Enforcer by [[Adjudicator Peton]].
- **Deepening Schism:** The realization of Dahmian's potential involvement in the camp murders makes the party suspect Dahmian. It's also curious why he has remained a Witness for so long; he seeks to escape the [[Truthkeepers]] but doesn't feel safe doing so.
