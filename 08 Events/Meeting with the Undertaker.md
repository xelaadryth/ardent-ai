# Overview
Following the invitation at the [[Whitespine's Fang]], the squad is led to the [[Bruckslats]] hideout in the [[Scarstone Quarter]] to meet the [[Undertaker]] (Mraize). The meeting shifts from a standard briefing to a personal crucible for [[N'tal]] and [[Fina]].

# The Meeting
The downstairs basement of the hideout is lush and trophy-filled, with what looks like vials of metallic powder, taxidermized creatures like mammals and birds, a case of pinned butterflies, and who knows what else in jars and display cases. Inanimate like rocks, metals, crystals, to living and dried plants. And animals for that matter, an exotic light green bird is actually alive and flutters down to the man's shoulder.

# The Background

The Ghostbloods had [[Doug (Guddy)]] and another Ghostblood to bring the paperwork to officially make a deal of trade and protection with N'tal's family. But he went rogue, and there ended up being a Bloodmarks deal with someone else instead. He produces a document that marks the command that authorizes the [[Bloodmarks]] to kill his family.

# The Revelation
The Undertaker points out that while the Bloodmarks used violence, they acted under a "legal" transfer authorized by the Nurian royal family. He slides the contract across the table, revealing the elegant but hurried signature of an Alethi proxy: [[Fina]] val Nuria. It becomes clear that Fina’s past negligence in signing documents without reading them provided the legal cover for the Bloodmarks to slaughter N'tal's kin and steal their mine.

The Undertaker also informs them that [[Arbiter Jalis]], the man who carried out the raid, is in the city and has been helping find [[Possessed]] to sell to the [[Truthkeepers]]. The Undertaker helped facilitate the deal but was not there in person.

For now, just causing trouble for the Bloodmarks is good enough. But if you can trace the source of the [[Nurian Gems]] for us, then even better.

If the party asks "What are the Ghostbloods' goals?", he answers: "Hmm, let's say our goal is establishing trade routes." If pressed: "But for now it's simply to work together on areas of mutual benefit as allies. Exchanging information. Fighting the [[Bloodmarks]]. It's not like we're inducting you as real members, unless you want to bear the mark of the [[Ghostbloods]]. Permanently."

How do you get to Mythos/Nuria - that's extremely expensive information to buy. "Let's talk about that in the future, but tracking the gems will surely lead you there as well. But I suspect it's west, somewhere in Jah Keved."
# Requests
- Mess with the [[Bloodmarks]].
- Find out where the [[Nurian Gems]] are coming from.
- Acquire rare and valuable objects; Shin grass is common, he's interested in a Nurian gem sample. Also interested in [[Zabriel]]'s sources ([[Bodenar]]).
# Outcomes
- **Fractured Trust:** The relationship between [[N'tal]] and [[Fina]] is strained by the revelation of her role in his family's tragedy.
- **Leaving the Nest:** Some time after the party departs the hideout, they find the Bloodmark squad, leading directly to [[The Foundry Chase]].
