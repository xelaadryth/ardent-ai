# Description

The party must figure out how to sneak through Mother's Gate, or fall back to battle plans in the [[Assault on Mother's Gate]].

# Setup

After the party bluffs their way through the docks, realize Mother's Gate has Johb's guards that recognize people on sight, and black sand at each bridge entrance. Black send turns black when it gets wet.

# Options

1. Fight their way through, lose their cover, maybe reveal that they are radiant (fight) - [[Assault on Mother's Gate]]
2. Ground isn't that deep (~10 ft), can create underpassages, use stormlight beads as ground and walls to keep out the other beads and Division from underneath (tactics)
3. Convince a guard to water the black sand and help them through (conversation)
4. Convince [[Johb]] to turn against [[Grand Justicar Malice]] and leave the [[Bloodmarks]], needs power to achieve his goal to bring power back to Scadrial. Convince him to join the [[Ghostbloods]] instead.
