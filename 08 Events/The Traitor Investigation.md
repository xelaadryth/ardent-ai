# Description
A tense internal investigation within the [[Knights of Dusk]] triggered by a series of security breaches. After the [[Stackhouses]] and [[Fieldwards]] locations were compromised, [[Valerius Atol]] has moved the core group to a temporary safehouse to identify a suspected traitor.

# Participants
- [[Valerius Atol]]: Lead Investigator.
- [[Beric Quinn]]: Participant (Knows location).
- [[Renna Palir]]: Suspect (Had physical notes on locations).
- [[Ardent Surusa]]: Suspect (True Traitor infiltrating via [[Lila]]).
- [[Otson]]: Suspect (Fieldwards cell member).
- [[Bellus]]: Suspect (Fieldwards cell member).
- [[Kaz]]: Informant (Reports on [[Stackhouses]] breach).
- [[Dahmian]]: Connection (Both he and [[Beric Quinn]] report to [[Ojog]]).

# The Setup
Upon arriving at the [[Stackhouses]], [[Valerius Atol]] redirected the party to a secondary, undisclosed location. He revealed that because the [[Truthkeepers]] have successfully raided multiple cells, a leak is certain. Notably, only the party, Valerius, [[Beric Quinn]], and the [[Fieldwards]] cell members ([[Otson]], [[Bellus]], Elara) were privy to the specific coordinates of the recent hideouts.

# Investigation Leads
- **Knowledge Distribution:** High-ranking members at HQ know the locations, but a compromise at that level would likely result in larger strikes against [[General Spade]]. 
- **Communication Methods:** How is the spy contacting the [[Truthkeepers]]? Evidence of spanreed use or physical drops must be sought.
- **The Ojog Connection:** Both [[Dahmian]] and [[Beric Quinn]] have historical reporting lines to [[Ojog]], though Beric's loyalty was proven at the [[Saving Jory at the Abandoned Docks]].
- **Compromised Stackhouses:** A report from [[Kaz]] confirms that the [[Stackhouses]] safehouse was compromised by units matching [[Senny]]'s description, which occurred after the [[Fieldwards]] cell was already in hiding, potentially clearing [[Otson]] and [[Bellus]] of that specific leak.

# Suspect Evidence

### [[Ardent Surusa]] (Traitor)
- **Physical Evidence:** She is secretly carrying a spanreed and specialized paper.
- **Brazier Scraps:** Her room at the [[Devotary of Insight]] contains a glyphward brazier with charred scraps of womenscript detailing rebel movements.
- **Scholar's Rest:** She was observed by [[Iiko]] removing rare books from [[Scholar's Rest]], presumably to prevent rebel access or to deliver them to [[High Enforcer Aylen]].

### [[Renna Palir]]
- **The Notes:** She possesses a ledger with the written locations of both safehouses. She claims these were for her personal logistical notes.
- **The Quills:** Inspection of her writing kit shows that her standard quill and her two spanreeds ([[Truthkeepers]] and [[Knights of Dusk]]) have all been used recently, making her timing unverifiable.

### [[Otson]] & [[Bellus]] (Red Herrings)
- **Suspicious Behavior:** They have been observed whispering together near [[Truthkeepers]] patrol routes.
- **The Map:** [[Otson]] was found with an un-coded map of [[Revolar]] hidden in his boot, though he claims it was for navigation during the [[Fieldwards]] escape.
- **Timeline:** They were rescued recently, providing a window where they could have been "turned" by [[Neval]] or [[Senny]].

# Outcomes
- Identification of [[Ardent Surusa]] as a [[Truthkeepers]] plant.
- Potential strain on the relationship between [[Renna Palir]] and the squad.
