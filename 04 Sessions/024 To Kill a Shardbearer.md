# Description
The [[Truthkeepers]] and Vamah forces launch a full-scale assault on the [[Knights of Dusk]] base in the [[Darkstone Forest]]. The squad defends an advance outpost, executes [[Adjudicator Peton]], and witnesses the rise of a new Shardbearer within the enemy ranks.

# Events
- **The Warning:** A wounded [[Beric Quinn]] returns to the base, warning that the march has begun. The [[Truthkeepers]] have identified him as a rebel spy.
- **The Advance Outpost:** [[General Spade]] establishes a forward position outside the main hideout. The squad scouts to the west, encountering [[Vamah Princedom]] soldiers herding prisoners as fodder.
- **The Pit Trap:** The party lures the enemy into a pit trap disguised with Tension-infused cloth and stone. Three soldiers fall as the prisoners are safely escorted across.
- **Scouting Skirmish:** During a bridge collapse involving [[N'tal]]'s Abrasion, the party eliminates a squad of soldiers, though two scouts escape. The freed prisoners take up spears to join the defense.
- **The Northern Front:** A runner alerts the squad to a breach from within and the presence of Shardbearers to the north. The party relocates to find [[Adjudicator Peton]], [[Ojog]], and [[Brightlord Juran]] cornering rebel militia.
- **Death of a Justice:** The squad engages the command group. After a brutal exchange, [[Adjudicator Peton]] is executed. [[Galeth-son-Thald]] is downed while attempting to claim Peton's Shardblade, incapacitated by the blade's spiritual screams.
- **The New Shardbearer:** [[Ojog]] cries out in grief and seizes Peton's Shardblade. As he touches the weapon, the stone sealing it disintegrates. He speaks the First Ideal: "Life before death..." and rejoins the fray as a Shardbearer.
- **The Bloodmark Sacrifice:** [[Tash The Hound]] arrives to reinforce the attackers. The [[Bloodmarks]] survivors [[Gorlo]], [[Piggy]], and [[Fang]] intervene on behalf of the party. [[Piggy]] and [[Fang]] are killed in a heroic effort to stall Tash.
- **The Escape:** The party utilizes [[N'tal]]'s Abrasion to slide the unconscious [[Galeth-son-Thald]] to safety. [[Gorlo]] is the sole survivor of the Bloodmark trio, fleeing alongside the squad into the depths of the forest.
