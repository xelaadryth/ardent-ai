# Description
The squad escapes the [[Silent Oath]] via a desperate transition into [[Shadesmar]], discovering stowaways and a missing companion upon their arrival in the Cognitive Realm.

# Events
- **The Ultimatum:** [[High Enforcer Aylen]] confronts the [[Deepen Light]] at the docks, listing the party's crimes. [[Varo Three-Knives]] attempts to extort the party for 500 marks for "escort services."
- **Hoid's Counsel:** Hidden in the secret storage compartment, [[Hoid]] advises the party to use the [[Nurian Gems]] to escape into the Cognitive Realm.
- **The Shattered Transition:** [[Kuma]] and [[Mallow]] activate the gems. The process drains their lifeforce until [[Kuma]] breaks the contact. The entire vessel and those on board—including [[Renna Palir]], [[Gorlo]], [[Varo Three-Knives]], and [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]]—are pulled into [[Shadesmar]].
- **Kuma's Absence:** Upon arrival, [[Kuma]] is nowhere to be found. [[Viscose]] reports that he is simply "gone."
- **The Hero of Ages:** The party discovers [[Kaizen]], a Scadrian youth and Suit of [[The Set]], hiding on the ship. He claims to be the "Hero of Ages" and the ship's true captain, demonstrating metal-manipulation via Allomancy.
- **Cognitive Sailing:** The party creates oars from ship railings. Using [[Iiko]]'s Division to create oarlocks and [[N'tal]]'s Abrasion to reduce friction, they begin rowing across the bead-sea.
- **Sighting:** After two days, the party spots a massive purple entity and a distant vessel, the *Wandering Eye*.
