# Description
The party breaks [[Tash The Hound]]'s pursuit with the assistance of [[Hoid]], and escapes to the [[Deepen Light]] where they question [[Hoid]], whose answers only serve to confuse them more.

# Events
- **Illusory Path**: To throw off the pursuit, the party turns a corner and [[Iiko]] conjured a fake road, while [[N'tal]] left a slippery patch there. [[Tash The Hound]] was thrown off by [[Hoid]] using "Yolish Lightweaving" to have another illusion flee into [[Iiko]]'s illusion.
- **Vines and Stone**: [[Fina]] grew plants to slow the pursuit, while [[Galeth-son-Thald]] sealed the way behind them, slowing and ultimately losing [[Tash The Hound]].
- **The Disc**: [[Hoid]] picks a metal disc off of [[Galeth-son-Thald]] and tosses it away
- **The Ship**: The party runs onto the ship, with [[Iiko]] claiming to be "John Bloodmark". [[Varo Three-Knives]] sees through the ruse but on the off-chance [[Iiko]] really is an "Array of the Set", lets them sail with them for a short while since they aren't causing any trouble. Also learning from [[N'tal]] that they have "local contacts" with Nuria intrigues him.
