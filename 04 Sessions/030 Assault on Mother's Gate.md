# Description
The squad, bolstered by new allies and reinforced naval power, strategizes for the liberation of [[Haka'alaku]] and the reopening of [[Mother's Gate]].

# Events
- **New Allies:** The squad welcomes [[Nolnin]], a Scadrian Ghostblood agent who has been instrumental in organizing the resistance. [[Viscose]] assists in a delicate operation to enhance [[Galeth-son-Thald]]'s combat prowess by implanting an iron Hemallurgic spike.
- **The Escape and Parley:** In a controversial move, [[Cadence]] orchestrates the release of [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]] and [[Kaizen]], arguing for their agency and freedom. [[Rissa]] leaves behind a note and a parley request. The party agrees to a conditional alliance: [[Rissa]] will aid in liberating [[Haka'alaku]] and getting them to [[Bodenar]] to confront [[Grand Justicar Malice]], while [[Kaizen]] joins them as a combatant.
- **The Mysterious Benefactor:** [[Gorlo]] reports meeting a man named "Atlas" in town—a soldier-like darkeyes with one red eye—who traded Stormlight spheres for dun spheres, showing a vested interest in the party's success.
- **Strategic Planning:** [[Rissa]] provides tactical intel on the six islands of [[Haka'alaku]]. She presents the party with the choice of a direct assault on [[Johb]] or bypassing the city to strike at [[Grand Justicar Malice]] directly in [[Bodenar]] after recovering Stormlight from highstorms.
- **The Growing Party:** The squad is joined by [[Nolnin]], [[Renna Palir]], [[Viscose]], [[Gorlo]], [[Rissa]], [[Kaizen]], [[Hysteresis]], [[Varo Three-Knives]], and [[Koloko]].
