# Description
The [[Truthkeepers]], [[Vamah Princedom]], and [[Bloodmarks]] launch a combined assault on the [[Darkstone Forest]] base. The party defends the fortifications in a desperate siege that forces the insurgency to evacuate while they flee into the bead-seas again.

# Events
- **The Betrayal and Looting:** Upon returning to base, the party discovered that darkeyed recruits had looted the stormlight gem stash and fled. Fighting and looting plagued the residential areas, and [[Bloodmarks]] had captured the front gatehouse before [[General Spade]] and [[Iiko]] reclaimed it.
- **The Siege:** The base was attacked by [[Tash The Hound]] (Bloodmarks), [[General Odrin Lesh]] and [[Brightlord Juran]] (Vamah), and [[Adjudicator Milara]] with [[Ojog]] (Truthkeepers).
- **Fortification Defense:** The party utilized roof-mounted ballistae to hold the line, cutting down siege ropes and decimating the Vamah chull-pulled battering ram, which [[Mallow]] sabotaged by sinking its wheels.
- **The Fall of Tash:** [[Tash The Hound]] was impaled and pinned by [[Galeth-son-Thald]] using stone spears and a ballista bolt.
- **Iiko's Illusions:** To prevent the Truthkeeper breach, [[Iiko]] used illusions of reinforcements and ballistae, effectively stalling the assault and forcing the Truthkeeper forces to surrender.
- **The Final Battle:** [[General Odrin Lesh]] challenged [[General Spade]] to a duel; [[General Spade]] disintegrated him using the surge of Division.
- **Ojog's Surrender:** [[Ojog]] surrendered after the Truthkeeper lines collapsed, refusing to renounce his loyalty to his order.
- **The Silent Oath:** Twenty Skybreakers of the [[Silent Oath]] descended upon the base. [[General Spade]] ordered an immediate evacuation via the escape tunnel.
- **The Escape:** The party boarded a boat provided by [[Nathaniel]] to reach safety. [[Istrum]] intercepted them, claiming she was there only to retrieve [[Ojog]] for the Skybreakers, whom she identified as the only true Knights Radiant. 
- **Revelation:** [[High Enforcer Aylen]] arrived, revealed as the Herald Nale (Nalan). He reduced the base to ash with Division as the party watched from the fleeing boat.
- **The Final Twist:** The party realized [[Varo Three-Knives]] was aboard their escape vessel (the Deepen Light). As they sailed away, Aylen turned his gaze upon them.

# Outcomes
- [[02 Knights of Dusk Arc]] concluded.
- The base at [[Darkstone Forest]] was destroyed.
- [[Ojog]] is in the custody of [[High Enforcer Aylen]].
- The party is currently aboard the [[Deepen Light]] with [[Varo Three-Knives]].
