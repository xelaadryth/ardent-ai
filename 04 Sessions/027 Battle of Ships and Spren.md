# Description
The party engages in a naval boarding action against a [[Bloodmarks]] vessel, resulting in the death of a nemesis and the discovery of enslaved spren.

# Events
- **The Boarding:** The *Wandering Eye*, commanded by [[Arbiter Jalis]], intercepts the party. [[Kaizen]] and [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]] use Allomancy to leap to the enemy ship. [[Mallow]] grabs Rissa, and the three end up aboard the enemy deck.
- **The Duel of Vengeance:** [[N'tal]] pursues [[Arbiter Jalis]]. During the struggle, [[Mallow]] stabs Rissa, wounding her severely. [[N'tal]] utilizes Division to disintegrate Jalis's arms.
- **Spren Frenzy:** The combat attracts predatory spren. Painspren swarm and kill the incapacitated [[Arbiter Jalis]]. Angerspren target [[Kaizen]], [[Mallow]], and Rissa, while [[Iiko]] attempts to de-escalate the violence.
- **Victory:** The party secures the ship as [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]] falls unconscious. [[Varo Three-Knives]] assists in the final cleanup once the spren are repelled.
- **The Oar-Slaves:** Investigating below decks, the party discovers a dozen [[Peakspren]] chained to the oars.
