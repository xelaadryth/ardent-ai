# Description
The squad successfully infiltrated [[Haka'alaku]] through [[Mother's Gate]], leveraging illusions and disguises to navigate the occupied city. They were joined by [[Atlas]], a Skybreaker squire sent by [[Istrum]] to monitor their actions, who chose to align with them against the [[Bloodmarks]]. During the infiltration, they heard a cryptic message from a missing [[Kuma]], confirming he is trapped in the Spiritual Realm.

# Events
- **Atlas's Arrival:** [[Atlas]] joined the party, revealing he is a Skybreaker squire under [[Istrum]], tasked with observing the party. He declared his intention to deliver judgment to the [[Bloodmarks]], aligning his goals with the squad.
- **Haka'alaku Infiltration:** The party, aboard a captured Set patrol ship (formerly Captain Harl's), utilized [[Iiko]]'s Illumination to impersonate [[Captain Harl]] and his original crew (with [[Hysteresis]] mimicking a crew member named [[Posha]]). The rest of the party donned uniforms as disguise.
- **Dock Encounter:** At the docks, an older Unkalaki woman named [[Warden]] greeted them, inquiring about the other two missing ships. The party bluffed, stating they were acquiring more supplies. As they unloaded crates containing their shattered [[Nurian Gems]], [[Warden]] briefly recognized [[Posha]], but [[Iiko]] swiftly created a distraction while [[Hysteresis]] (as Posha) remained unresponsive.
- **Mother Island Sealed:** When the party indicated moving towards [[Mother's Gate]], [[Warden]] informed them that Mother Island was sealed due to a recent disturbance.
- **Island Reconnaissance:** The party proceeded through Dock Island, then Craft/Slave Island (noting [[Peakspren]] constructing ships), and Garrison Island. While on Garrison Island, [[Iiko]] infiltrated [[Captain Harl]]'s room, collecting personal items including a Branzah plant (native to the Shattered Plains), a fashion folio, and a map of Roshar.
- **The Thousand Eyes Deal:** On the Island of a Thousand Eyes, the party encountered an Unkalaki named [[Yellow]], who expressed surprise at the early arrival of the [[Nurian Gems]]. [[Yellow]] contacted [[Innik]], a Malwish administrator, whom the party convinced that they had recovered the gems from rebels. [[Innik]] then issued them a quick contract to deliver the [[Nurian Gems]] to [[Bodenar]].
- **Mother's Gate Breach:** Approaching [[Mother's Gate]], the party noticed black sand that would detect their Lightweaving illusions. They adapted by placing their spren in crates and relying solely on their uniforms to bluff their way past the guards.
- **Kuma's Plea:** As they passed through [[Mother's Gate]], they heard [[Kuma]]'s voice calling out for [[Mallow]], revealing he was trapped in the Spiritual Realm and urging them to find his mother and a way to free him.
- **Unkalaki Patrol:** Upon emerging to the other side of the gate, the party observed several Unkalaki moving towards them, indicating potential immediate detection or confrontation.

# Outcomes
- The party gained a new ally in [[Atlas]].
- Confirmed [[Kuma]]'s location in the Spiritual Realm and received new objectives to find him and [[Higuma]].
- Successfully navigated initial infiltration of [[Haka'alaku]] and acquired a delivery contract for [[Nurian Gems]].
- Identified key Set personnel in [[Haka'alaku]]: [[Warden]], [[Yellow]], and [[Innik]].
- Collected intel on [[Captain Harl]] and the local environment.
