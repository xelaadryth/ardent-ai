# Description
The squad navigates the political minefield of Revolar, balancing their cover within the Truthkeepers against their deepening allegiance to the Knights of Dusk. The session concludes with a high-stakes rescue in the Skyglass District following a suspicious arson at the Highlord's estate.

# Events
- **Scholar's Rest Inquiry:** [[Iiko]] continues researching the path of an Ardent and the Devotary of Sincerity. [[Ardent Kaelis]] expresses concern over the missing mythological texts, noting his friend [[Ardent Vedelis]]—who had a copy of "Words of Radiance"—remains missing from his northern trip. The party realizes with horror that the Vedelis they killed near [[Hearthstone]] was the same Ardent Kaelis seeks.
- **Reporting to the Archivum:** The squad reports the findings of the Shield Ring warehouse breach to the [[Truthkeepers]] in the [[Vamah Archivum]], presenting the writ while conveniently omitting their personal involvement.
- **The Skyglass Distraction:** Executing a plan for the [[Knights of Dusk]], the squad uses their investigation writ to summon the Shield Ring guards for interrogation, effectively clearing the path for rebel maneuvers. 
- **The Cinder Evacuation:** A fire erupts at [[Highlord Paladar]]'s manor in the [[Skyglass District]]. The party arrives to find survivors, including [[Jory Spadis]], who helps them rescue trapped servants. [[Galeth-son-Thald]] scales the manor to save them. The servants reveal the fire was an accident during a botched "extermination" crew job, which was meant to be secret to avoid embarrassment—specifically regarding a missing picture book of [[Chip]] (Archisal Paladar), the Highlord's neglected youngest son. The party realizes Paladar's secret, desperate love for his lost son.

# Outcomes
- Party identifies [[Ardent Vedelis]] as the Ardent they killed.
- Successfully executed a diversion for the [[Knights of Dusk]].
- Discovered [[Highlord Paladar]]'s hidden paternal grief for his son, [[Chip]].
