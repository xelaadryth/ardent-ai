# Description
The squad prepares for the rescue of [[Jory Spadis]] by scouting the western [[Slipmarket]] docks, securing specialized communication equipment, and identifying the prison ship [[Deepen Light]].

# Events
- **The Devotary Visit:** The party visited the [[Vorin Devotaries|Devotary of Sincerity]] to seek word from [[Dahmian]]. [[Ardent Erna]] delivered a cryptic message from him asking "how do I find you," though he left no contact information himself. 
- **Logistical Prep:** The party purchased three pairs of spanreeds from [[Ardent Erna]] for 240 marks (40 marks contributed per player).
- **Harbormaster Inquiry:** In the western [[Slipmarket]], the squad consulted [[Harbormaster Ves]]. While he was protective of cargo manifests, he identified ships for hire: the *Green Mercy* (40 marks/day) and the dilapidated *Windraiser*. 
- **The Deepen Light:** The party identified the [[Deepen Light]], a ship departing tonight. Using Division, they surreptitiously inspected the hull and confirmed the presence of [[Nurian Gems]] in the hold. They noted the crew, led by [[Gath]], were highly efficient but suspicious of the party's observations.
- **Recruiting Irregulars:** The squad rescued and hired two local children, [[Litsen]] and [[Mazie]], after they were chased by red-armband guard patrols. The children were paid to monitor the docks for [[Bloodmarks]] movement and confirmed the syndicate's headquarters is likely in the center of the district. [[Mallow]] provided them with fresh fish.
- **Meeting the Smuggler:** Guided by Truthkeeper intel, [[Renna Palir]] facilitated a meeting with [[Varo Three-Knives]] at a local tavern. Despite his profession, he appeared friendly. He revealed that the interrogation schedule for the "[[Possessed]]" prisoner had been moved up to "shortly."
- **Syndicate Intelligence:** [[Varo Three-Knives]] confirmed that his direct superior is [[Arbiter Rissa Vale]].
