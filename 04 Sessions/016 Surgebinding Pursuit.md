# Description
The squad navigates a series of emotional and physical trials in [[Revolar]], leading to the speaking of the First Ideal by [[N'tal]] during a high-stakes confrontation with [[Arbiter Jalis]] at an abandoned foundry.

# Events
- **The Disappearance of [[Jory Spadis]]:** Following the fire at the Paladar manor, the squad notices [[Jory Spadis]] muttering to himself before he mysteriously vanishes.
- **The Sincerity Detour:** [[Iiko]] visits the [[Devotary of Sincerity]], meeting **Head Ardent Erna**. She provides them with a *Book of Endless Pages* and reveals that the [[Truthkeepers]] are systematically "disappearing" books on mythology and philosophy. She confirms that the late [[Ardent Vedelis]] possessed their only copy of *Words of Radiance*.
- **Reunion at the Stackhouses:** The party checks in with the [[Knights of Dusk]]. [[Mallow]] attempts to reunite with [[Khria]], who remains cold and distant. [[Khria]] reveals she knows about the death of [[Ardent Vedelis]] and [[Lavrik]] at the hands of the party, as she received their final spanreed message.
- **The Darkstone Journey:** [[Khria]] leads the party to the [[Knights of Dusk]] HQ in the [[Darkstone Forest]]. During the trek, she confronts [[Mallow]] about the past, blaming her sister's "betrayal" for her capture by the [[Truthkeepers]]. She warns the party that joining the insurgency is a one-way path.
- **Audience with [[General Spade]]:** The General receives the party, learning of the deaths of his men. Despite his grief, he recognizes the party's potential and begins explaining the nature of the [[Orders of the Knights Radiant]]. He identifies [[Iiko]] as a Truthwatcher and explains the surges of Progression and Illumination before being interrupted by reports that the [[Bloodmarks]] were moving a prisoner that night, and the Knights of Dusk immediately mobilize, including the party.
- **The Hateful Hour Pursuit:** The squad stakes out [[Sun's Zenith]] and follows a group of [[Bloodmarks]] thugs carrying a prisoner. The pursuit through the [[Scarstone Quarter]] and [[Cremling Quarter]] forces the party to manifest their surges:
    - [[Iiko]] used Illumination to light the path.
    - [[N'tal]] utilized Division to collapse a fence.
    - [[Kuma]] attempted Transformation (Soulcasting) to bypass a wagon.
    - [[Fina]] used Progression to weaponize a cabbage.
    - [[N'tal]] used Abrasion to navigate slippery crem-sludge.
- **[[The Foundry Chase]] Climax:** At an abandoned foundry, [[Arbiter Jalis]] attempts to drown the prisoner in a crem-vat. [[N'tal]] speaks the First Ideal, choosing to save the prisoner over taking revenge on Jalis. His words are accepted. He utilizes Division and Abrasion to rescue the captive, who is revealed to be [[Zabriel]], not Jory.
- **Resolution:** [[Arbiter Jalis]] escapes into a highstorm. [[Zabriel]] promises to repay the party, citing his legendary luck as his only remaining asset.
