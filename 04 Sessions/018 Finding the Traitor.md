# Description
The squad navigates a high-stakes internal investigation at [[Valerius Atol]]'s penthouse, resulting in the identification of [[Ardent Surusa]] as a spy, a violent confrontation with a [[Truthkeepers]] strike team, and a fundamental shift in [[Senny]]'s loyalty.

# Events
- **The Penthouse Meeting:** [[Valerius Atol]] summons the suspects to his luxurious penthouse overlooking the [[Stackhouses]]. He oversees the investigation to root out the mole compromising the [[Knights of Dusk]].
- **Exposing the Traitor:** Suspicion falls on [[Ardent Surusa]] due to her possession of blank paper without writing implements. [[Kaz]] confirms her suspicious movements during the [[The Fieldwards Purge]], leading to the discovery of her spanreed and burned communications in her room. It is confirmed she has been reporting to the [[Truthkeepers]].
- **The Raid:** A Truthkeeper team led by [[Senny]] assaults the smaller Stackhouses hideout. The party confronts them, and [[Valerius Atol]] reveals his identity to his former friend, [[Senny]].
- **The Second Ideal:** [[Iiko]] manifests the surge of Illumination to show [[Senny]] the truth behind the [[Truthkeepers]]' atrocities. [[Iiko]] speaks the second ideal of the Truthwatchers: "I will bring truth to those who have been deceived."
- **Resolution:** The Truthkeeper squad is bound. [[Valerius Atol]] advises the party that the insurgency's next objective is to strip [[Highlord Paladar]] and [[General Odrin Lesh]] of their lighteyed support. [[Senny]] mentions that he heard [[Adjudicator Peton]] was to interrogate [[Jory Spadis]] at the abandoned docks in the west side of the [[Slipmarket]] the next night.
