# Description
The squad is summoned by Witness Dahmian for a final evaluation mission to secure his promotion to Enforcer. The encounter forces the party to confront the reality of Truthkeeper justice and the personal history of their newest associate.

# Events
- **[[Dahmian's Promotion Trial]]:** Witness Dahmian leads the squad to a corner of the [[Slipmarket]] to execute a suspected [[Possessed]]. The party recognizes Dahmian from various past lives in Nuria, leading to internal tension and suspicion.
- **The Catalyst for Defection:** The brutality of the trial and Dahmian's clinical detachment sets the stage for the party's eventual decision to aid the [[Knights of Dusk]] at the abandoned docks later that evening.
