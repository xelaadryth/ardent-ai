Following are some ideas and suggestions that you can choose to use or not:

To integrate the rescue of [Zabriel](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md) into the [Allegiance at the Abandoned Docks](obsidian://open?file=08%20Events%2FAllegiance%20at%20the%20Abandoned%20Docks.md) event, you can frame his presence as a consequence of his failed business dealings and his unique "knack" for sensing the obscure [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]

### Updating the Event

You can modify the event to reflect that the [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) are using the abandoned docks not just for [Jory Spadis](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FJory%20Spadis.md), but as a temporary holding pen for "assets" they intend to liquidate or trade.

**Why Zabriel is there:**

- **The Debt Trap:** Zabriel, having failed to sell his high-value [Nurian Gems](obsidian://open?file=11%20Lore%2FNurian%20Gems.md) specimen and falling into deep debt, was abducted by the [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)] They are holding him at the docks to squeeze him for information on his suppliers or to use him as a "broker" to fence other stolen goods, as he is known to have a knack for finding interesting people and obscure items [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]
- **The Hand-off:** While the [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) usually operate independently, they are only handing off Jory, Zabriel just happens to be there

### Zabriel’s Reward

Since the [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) have confiscated his stock, Zabriel cannot offer material wealth. Instead, his value lies in his connections and his "knack" [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)] Upon being saved, he could provide:

- **The "Weird" Lead:** Zabriel could reveal the location of a hidden cache of "curiosities" he stashed before his arrest—items that are not necessarily valuable in marks, but are highly prized by the [Undertaker](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FUndertaker.md) or useful for the party's research into the Cosmere [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]
- **The Syndicate Connection:** He could provide the party with the specific "call and answer" or secret meeting times for other [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) operations in the [Slipmarket](app://obsidian.md/Slipmarket), having overheard them while held captive.
- **The "Lucky" Tip:** Given his one good trait is being lucky and sensing the obscure, he could point the party toward a specific, overlooked location in [Revolar](obsidian://open?file=06%20Locations%2FRevolar.md) that holds a secret related to the [Knights of Dusk](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FKnights%20of%20Dusk.md) or the [Truthkeepers](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FTruthkeepers.md)' logistics, acting as a guide for their next move [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]

### Suggested Narrative Integration

- **The Rescue:** During the infiltration of the docks, the party finds Zabriel in a separate holding cell or tied up near the main interrogation area. Saving him adds a layer of urgency to the escape, as he is physically dejected and weary, requiring the party to protect him while they extract [Jory Spadis](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FJory%20Spadis.md) [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]
- **The Aftermath:** Once safe in the [Darkstone Forest](obsidian://open?file=06%20Locations%2FDarkstone%20Forest.md), Zabriel’s dejected state contrasts with his ambition. He might express a desire to become a "real" merchant to win back his parents' affection, offering his services as an information broker for the party in exchange for their protection from the [Bloodmarks](obsidian://open?file=05%20Factions%2FBloodmarks.md) [[1](obsidian://open?file=03%20NPCs%2FZabriel.md)]
