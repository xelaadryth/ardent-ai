In Session 33, the party fought Johb, who used his lurcher (iron pulling) powers to pull himself back to the guardhouse, while also yanking all metal objects from the players, leaving a bunch of small metal objects scattered over the ground, including Kuma's fabrial which he snatched to examine. 7 Shin guards attacked the party as well wielding large swords with uncanny skill. Iiko with their shardblade was the initial target, as they attacked with cries of "Shardbearer!" After Iiko started summoning illusions of themself though, those cries turned to "Honorbearer!" Galeth recognized one of the guards as Tarsht-son-Naszt, one of the Shin that was in his unit before Galeth abandoned them. The Shin guards were skilled, actively dodging the player's attacks, feinting, and even using a whirlwind slash to disperse some of Iiko's illusions. Johb tossed a burst fabrial that knocked over Galeth, and also was skilled at throwing numerous knives at once. He also took out a wooden shield, which was the cue for his Shin guards to all scatter to the side as he ripped all the knives back through the party from behind. He ultimately got hit by Galeth's warhammer and N'tal's division, leaving such a close call that he surrendered and told the guards stand down. When interrogated, he revealed that he gave in because the party was stronger and proved the victor, which means they got to decide what happens to him. Just like how he was in charge when he took control of Mother's Gate. He also revealed that he wasn't burn a Misting; he was turned into one through deadly experimentation by his Ghostblood parents, and turned to show a steel spike in his spine that gave him his lurcher powers. He left the Ghostbloods in disgust when he found out that someone had to die to give him his powers, and sought out Roshar as a source of powers that anyone can use, instead of needing to be born with it, leading to his fascination with fabrials. The party asked why he enslaved the peakspren, and he appeared shocked to find out that they were sentient, and not just reflections of human thoughts; he thought them the same as the common naturespren and emotionspren trapped in fabrials, and not as thinking people. He was also unwilling to directly go against Malice as she had bested him in the past, but he was willing to withdraw Set/Bloodmarks forces out of the Physical Realm and into Shadesmar, and withdraw out of Haka'alaku, returning it to the original people's rule.
