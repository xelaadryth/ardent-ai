# Description
A short Nurian merchant passing as Alethi, armed with knives and beginning the path of a Dustbringer.

# Personality
Distrustful and cautious, valuing patience and self-control to keep [[Pyre]] in check. Following his first oath, he is beginning to prioritize the protection of life over personal vengeance.

# Background
N'tal survived the [[Bloodmarks]] raid on his family mine in Nuria. In [[023 Preparing for the Siege]], he convinced [[Chip]] to defect/divulge info on [[The Set]], learning that the [[Ghostbloods]] want the party's help to dismantle their rivals.

# Goals
- Hunt down [[Arbiter Jalis]].
- Dismantle the operations of [[The Set]] and [[Grand Justicar Malice]].
- Protect the innocent from [[Bloodmarks]] exploitation.
