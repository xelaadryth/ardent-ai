# Description
Manic Nurian Truthwatcher and Envoy seeking fundamental truths and a place within the Ardentia.

# Background
In [[023 Preparing for the Siege]], Iiko disguised themselves as [[Istrum]] to infiltrate the [[Vamah Archivum]]. They met [[High Enforcer Aylen]] and learned that the [[Silent Oath]] is gathering legal evidence against the Radiants. They successfully recovered and studied several confiscated books intended for burning.

> Strength before weakness. To know strength, you must know truth. The truth of the past reshapes who is strong and weak. Sharing that truth and knowledge is strength, whereas keeping it away from everyone is weakness.

> Journey before destination. Learning is a never-ending journey, there's always another secret.
