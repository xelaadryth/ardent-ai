# Overview
In Vorinism, the primary form of worship is the pursuit of one's **Calling** and **Glory**. Every person of status (and many darkeyes) chooses a **Devotary**, an organization of Ardents dedicated to specific aspects of the Almighty's nature. These organizations provide spiritual guidance and administrative support across [[Alethkar]] and [[Jah Keved]].

# The Calling and Glory
A Vorin's spiritual life is defined by their interaction with the Almighty through their daily labor. 

- **Calling**: A person's chosen profession or focus in life (e.g., soldier, merchant, scholar).
- **Glory**: The spiritual attribute one aims to cultivate through their work. Examples mentioned in religious texts include:
    - **Glory of Knowledge**
    - **Glory of Strength**
    - **Glory of Charity**
    - **Glory of Song**
    - **Glory of Grace**
    - **Glory of Art**
    - **Glory of Wit**
    - **Glory of Determination**
    - **Glory of Purity**
    - **Glory of Honor**

# The Major Devotaries

## 1. Devotary of Sincerity
Focused on learning and the pursuit of objective truth. They utilize the *Book of Endless Pages*, which encourages questioning and the refinement of Vorin tenets. This devotary is noted for its willingness to allow Ardents to engage in field research. Notable members include [[Ardent Kaelis]] and the late [[Ardent Vedelis]].

## 2. Devotary of Purity
Dedicated to honesty, religious orthodoxy, and the study of natural history. They emphasize the inherent order of the physical world as a reflection of the Almighty.

## 3. Devotary of Insight
Focused on the rigorous study, translation, and interpretation of ancient and modern texts. They often work closely with scribes and historians.

## 4. Devotary of Denial
A group practicing asceticism, self-denial, and simplicity, believing that removing worldly distractions brings one closer to the Divine.

## 5. Devotary of Mind
Devoted to the study of history, linguistics, and literature. They are the primary custodians of cultural heritage in many Alethi cities.

## 6. Devotary of Mercy
Focused on the care of the troubled, the poor, and the helpless. They manage many of the infirmaries and orphanages within the city wards.

## 7. Devotary of Kalak
Specialized in the making of judgments, legal decisions, and the resolution of complex disputes between noble houses.

## 8. Order of Talenelat
A devotary primarily favored by the commonfolk; they focus on practical service, manual labor, and generally dislike complex theological debate.

# Distinct Organizations
- [[Veristitalians]]: While scholarly in nature, the Veristitalians are an independent movement and are **not** an official Vorin Devotary. They prioritize historical accuracy ("the truth of what happened") over religious dogma, though individual members often maintain membership in a devotary like Sincerity or Mind.
