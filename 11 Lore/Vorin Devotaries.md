# Overview
In Vorinism, the primary form of worship is the pursuit of one's **Calling** and **Glory**. Every person of status (and many darkeyes) chooses a **Devotary**, an organization of Ardents dedicated to specific aspects of the Almighty's nature. These organizations provide spiritual guidance and administrative support across [[Alethkar]] and [[Jah Keved]].

# The Calling and Glory
A Vorin's spiritual life is defined by their interaction with the Almighty through their daily labor. 

- **Calling**: A person's chosen profession or focus in life (e.g., soldier, merchant, scholar).
- **Glory**: The spiritual attribute one aims to cultivate through their work. Examples mentioned in religious texts include:
    - **Glory of Knowledge**
    - **Glory of Strength**
    - **Glory of Charity**
    - **Glory of Song**
    - **Glory of Grace**
    - **Glory of Art**
    - **Glory of Wit**
    - **Glory of Determination**
    - **Glory of Purity**
    - **Glory of Honor**

# The Major Devotaries

## 1. Devotary of Sincerity
Dedicated to seeking truth in all things. Members believe that there is always something to learn, and everything is subject to scrutiny, even their own religion. No question is rebuked. It is one of the smallest devotaries.

They utilize the *Book of Endless Pages*, which encourages questioning and the refinement of Vorin tenets.

This devotary is noted for its willingness to allow Ardents to engage in field research. Notable members include [[Ardent Kaelis]] and the late [[Ardent Vedelis]]. Head Ardent Erna.

## 2. Devotary of Purity
Dedicated to wholesomeness and honesty. Members of this devotary are expected to keep their thoughts and actions 'pure'. A member's talents are expected to be dedicated to purity as well, such as an artist depicting only religious topics.

## 3. Devotary of Insight
Dedicated to the study of Vorin religious texts. Many ardents from the Devotary of Insight are seen exchanging dulled lamps from the Palanaeum and putting infused ones in their place. They have a temple in Kholinar.

## 4. Devotary of Denial
Seems to emphasize the denial of desires to the point of asceticism.

## 5. Devotary of Mind
A monastic devotary with its home at the on the western slopes of the Horneater Peaks. It is devoted to historical and linguistic study, with the focus on literature

## 6. Devotary of Mercy
A devotary with a presence in Urithiru. Their ardents care for those who have trouble with their minds.

## 7. Devotary of Kelek
Helps those seeking to make difficult judgments or decisions. The devotary has a presence in Urithiru. Their ardents will take commissions for prayers and glyphwards.

## 8. Order of Talenelat
Related to the Herald Talenelat and, likely, the attributes ascribed to him by Vorinism. Those dedicated to this particular devotary are said to have a dislike for theological debates. It is viewed by some as an order for lesser people. The Order of Talenelat maintains a presence in western , adjacent to the temple of Talenelat. [[Dalinar]] was a follower of this devotary prior to his excommunication.
