# Overview
In Vorinism, the primary form of worship is the pursuit of one's **Calling** and **Glory**. Every person of status (and many darkeyes) chooses a **Devotary**, an organization of Ardents dedicated to specific aspects of the Almighty's nature.

# The Calling and Glory
- **Calling**: A person's chosen profession.
- **Glory**: The spiritual attribute cultivated through work.

# The Major Devotaries

## 1. Devotary of Sincerity
Dedicated to seeking truth. Smallest devotary. Utilizes the *Book of Endless Pages*. 
- **Head Ardent Erna**: Leads the Revolar office; manages a small, cramped space and protects books on mythology and philosophy from censorship.
- **Notable Members**: [[Ardent Kaelis]], [[Ardent Vedelis]].

## 2. Devotary of Purity
Dedicated to wholesomeness and honesty.

## 3. Devotary of Insight
Dedicated to the study of Vorin religious texts. Adheres strictly to the literal word of scripture.
- **Notable Members**: [[Ardent Surusa]].

## 4. Devotary of Denial
Emphasizes asceticism.

## 5. Devotary of Mind
Horneater Peaks-based devotary focused on literature.

## 6. Devotary of Mercy
Presence in Urithiru; cares for the mentally ill.

## 7. Devotary of Kelek
Helps with difficult judgments.

## 8. Order of Talenelat
Followers of Talenelat; dislike theological debate.
