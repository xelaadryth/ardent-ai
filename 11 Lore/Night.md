# Description
The Night was one of the three Bondsmith spren of Roshar, but left for Mythos, influencing it with the magic of Cultivation, without the bonds and oaths of Honor.
# History
Long ago, when Cultivation arrived on Roshar, she resonated with the Night's song that convinced her to stay on Roshar despite her original plan to find an uninhabited planet to settle on. The singers claimed the Wind and Stone, as well as spren, as their gods. As a result, the Night left Roshar to go to Mythos. Legends say that her tears for an ailing Mythian woman praying for her stillborn child when no one was listening granted an immaculate conception, and that child was loved and worshipped by all and became the founder of Nuria to protect it from the Fused.
