# Description

17 co-conspirators shattered Adonalsium into 16 shards using 4 dawnshards. Each dawnshard powers and limits 4 of the shards:

- INTEND
	- Ambition
	- Reason
	- Whimsy
	- Odium
- DEFINE
	- Devotion
	- Autonomy
	- Dominion
	- Virtuosity
- CHANGE
	- Invention
	- Cultivation
	- Endowment
	- Ruin
- EXIST
	- Preservation
	- Valor
	- Honor
	- Mercy
