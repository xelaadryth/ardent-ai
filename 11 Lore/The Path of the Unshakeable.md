# Overview
This character arc documents the journey of [[Galeth-son-Thald]] from a broken exile to a "Resolver" of the Ninth Order.

# Arc Phases

## Phase 1: The Resolver of Revolar ([[02 Knights of Dusk Arc]])
Galeth must realize that being "dependable" isn't just about following orders; it's about being the stone that others lean on when the world collapses. 
- **Key Event:** During [[The Darkstone Siege]], Galeth must choose to protect a group of fleeing civilians against the [[Truthkeepers]] he once served.
- **Potential Second Oath:** "I will stand where others fall."

## Phase 2: The Shield of the Innocent ([[03 Stormblessed Arc]])
Galeth encounters [[Tien]], the "Stormblessed." Seeing a young boy who protects others through beauty and empathy challenges Galeth's belief that he is a monster. He must protect [[Dillind]] from the recruitment drives in [[Tomat]].
- **Key Event:** Facing [[Amaram]]'s forces to ensure no more children are sacrificed for a "higher cause."

## Phase 3: The Truth of the Shepherd ([[05 Kharbranth Infiltration Arc]])
Galeth reunites with [[Szeth-son-son-Vallano]] in [[Jah Keved]]. Seeing the shepherd boy he once knew transformed into the Assassin in White forces Galeth to confront his past in Shinovar.
- **Key Event:** A confrontation with [[Meki-son-Vara]] in [[Bodenar]], where Galeth must resolve his guilt over the girl's death not by seeking death, but by stopping Meki's current atrocities.
- **Potential Third Oath:** "I will resolve the conflicts I allowed to grow."

# Theme
**Restoration vs. Retribution.** Galeth seeks retribution (punishment) for himself, but the spren [[Kun'ahu]] seeks restoration (fixing). The arc concludes when Galeth accepts that he is not a monster, but a builder who once let his tools break.
