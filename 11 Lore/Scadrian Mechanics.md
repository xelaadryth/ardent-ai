# Overview
The [[Bloodmarks]] worldhopper branch utilizes the Metallic Arts of Scadrial—Allomancy and Feruchemy—to survive and dominate within [[Shadesmar]].

# Allomancy
Allomancers burn specific metals to gain temporary physical or cognitive abilities. 
- **Steel/Iron:** Used by corsairs to push or pull on the metal fasteners of Rosharan vessels.
- **Zinc/Brass:** Used by leaders to manipulate the emotions of spren and humans alike.
- **Tin:** Used by scouts to see through the mists of the Cognitive Realm.

# Feruchemy
Feruchemists store attributes in metal jewelry for later use. Unlike Surgebinding, these powers do not require Stormlight.
- **Steel (Speed):** Used by [[Grand Justicar Malice]] to achieve supernatural reflexes.
- **Pewter (Strength):** Used by boarding parties to overpower Rosharan defenders.
- **Gold (Health):** Allows the syndicate's elite to survive wounds that would kill others.
