# Description
A disputed legal document involving the ownership of the Aelwood Forest. Originally the ancestral land of [[N'tal]]'s merchant family, it was declared "disputed" and transferred to House Morivanter following administrative neglect and the intervention of the [[Bloodmarks]].

# Implications
- The contract was facilitated by Fina's family signing authority, providing the legal cover used by the Bloodmarks to seize the land.
- The Undertaker used this information to align [[N'tal]]'s interests with the [[Ghostbloods]], leveraging [[N'tal]]'s vendetta against [[Arbiter Jalis]] and the Bloodmarks.

# Connections
- [[N'tal]]: Original family claimant.
- [[Fina]]: Indirectly responsible for the legal transfer via past administrative negligence.
- [[Bloodmarks]]: Beneficiaries of the land seizure.
