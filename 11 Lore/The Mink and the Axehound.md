# Description
- There once was a tasty rabbi- uh a mink named Munchy who lived in a briar patch.
- Life was good and there were plenty of veggies to hunt, but every week, a hungry axehound named Snarls would come and take one of the minks away.
- Now most minks would try to hide, but if they were caught they would freeze in place, let themselves be carried off, and resign themselves to Snarls's cooking pot.
- But one day when Snarls trotted by to choose his dinner, Munchy saw that it was her friend that was lifted by the scruff of her neck.
- Munchy was scared, but tired of hiding. She leaped out of the brush, three pounds of fur and fury. She BIT ol' Snarls square on the nose. She THUMPED ol' Snarls square in the eye. But that was all she could do.
- Snarls yelped, Snarls snarled, but still he trotted off with her doomed friend to the simmering cooking pot for another tasty meal.
- "I better find a different briar patch" ol' Snarls thought to himself, "health insurance is expensive these days, these stitches will cost a fortune." I mean, uh, he thought "Ow."
- But while that could have been the end of it, Munchy, seeing her other friends cowering in fear and grief, swore vengeance, and devised ... a *plan*.
- The next week, Snarls caught a mink from a different briar patch, but when he was about to plop him in the cooking pot, out of the brush leaped three pounds of fur and fury.
- Munchy BIT ol' Snarls square on the nose. She THUMPED ol' Snarls square in the eye. Right where she wanted, right next to the cooking pot. And in the confusion, the axehound tumbled in, and the other mink sprinted away. Munchy, proud of a job well done, started to head home, but hid when more axehounds stumbled upon the fragrant stew.
- Now these axehounds were confused at the choice of meat in the pot, but they ate well that night. Turns out ol' Snarls was tasty too. And that could have been the end of it.
- But suddenly, out of the brush leaped three pounds of fur and fury. Munchy BIT one of the axehounds square on the nose. She THUMPED another square in the eye. But there was nothing she could do about the rest, and she was tossed into the cooking pot.
- Now these axehounds had never tasted mink before, so when they found out Munchy was even tastier than ol' Snarls, they tracked the minks' tracks back to their briar patches, and within a month not a single mink was left uneaten.
- The End!
