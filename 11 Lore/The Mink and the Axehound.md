# Description
- There once was a tasty rabbi- uh a mink named Munchy who lived in a briar patch.
- Life was good and there were plenty of veggies to hunt, but every week, a hungry axehound named Snarls would come and take one of the minks away.
- Now most minks would try to hide, but if they were caught they would freeze in place, let themselves be carried off, and resign themselves to Snarls's cooking pot.
- But one day when Snarls trotted by to choose his dinner, Munchy saw that it was her friend that was lifted by the scruff of her neck.
- Munchy was scared, but tired of hiding. She leaped out of the brush, four pounds of fur and fury. She BIT ol' Snarls square on the nose. She THUMPED ol' Snarls square in the eye. But that was all she could do.
- Snarls yelped, Snarls snarled, but still he trotted off with her doomed friend to the simmering cooking pot for another tasty meal.
- "I better find a different briar patch" ol' Snarls thought to himself, "health insurance is expensive these days, these stitches will cost a fortune."
- But while that could have been the end of it, Munchy, seeing her other friends cowering in fear and grief, swore vengeance and devised a plan.
- The next week, Snarls caught a mink from a different briar patch, but when he was about to plop him in the cooking pot, out of the brush leaped four pounds of fur and fury.
- Munchy BIT ol' Snarls square on the nose. She THUMPED ol' Snarls square in the eye. And in the confusion, the two minks and the axehound all tumbled into the cooking pot.
- When the other axehounds came by they were confused, but ate well that night. Turns out ol' Snarls was tasty too.
